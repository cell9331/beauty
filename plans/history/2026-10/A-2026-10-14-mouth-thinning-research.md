## Hypothesis and finite experiment

The current negative-fullness path uses a smooth minimum of source/anchor
distances, followed by a bilinear lattice and shared-edge scalar minorant.
A continuous analytic distance envelope may recover amplitude lost by that
representation while preserving the same zero regions and global gradient
and displacement limits. It does not remove the fixed oral-band geometric
constraint and is not expected, without evidence, to reach the reference.

Freeze one source-defined formula before rendering; no output-driven coefficient,
window, protection or threshold search. Representation/correctness repairs are
recorded separately; a failed effect is retained and rejected. If the one
candidate misses the original reference range, record that the requested
reference breakthrough was not demonstrated, even if the original incremental
improvement gate passes. No production integration or installation is included
in this research decision.
