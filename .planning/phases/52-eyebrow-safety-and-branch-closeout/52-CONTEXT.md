- Freeze all seven effective caps at exact `0.25`, matching the already accepted Phase 51 public-facade output cases; change only provisional ownership/comments unless an executable safety row exposes a real defect.

- Keep existing brow-relative displacement/radius formulas and their finite/unit-bound checks. Provider maximum acceptance must agree with the final cap and must not introduce a larger renderer-only or provider-only range.

### Exhaustive Lifecycle and Local Failure
- Use one typed seven-field descriptor matrix spanning public input, effective key path, named provider emission, signed/positive semantics, exact fresh cap, exact reused half-strength `0.125`, and the narrowest missing/malformed/provider-empty fixture.

- Valid eyebrow siblings and unrelated face/eye/nose/mouth/color/filter domains continue when one eyebrow field is removed; facade output extent and aggregate-only warnings/metrics remain stable and redacted.

### Exact Combined Convergence
- Make the final complete geometry oracle exactly 44 unique named fields: nine face/chin, fourteen eye, seven eyebrow, six nose, and eight mouth fields.

- Require at most 44 monotone provider-eligibility removals with no re-entry, alternate bound, or second conflict pass. Every final nonzero effective field must have a nonempty named emission and appear exactly once in aggregate metrics and unified dispatch.
