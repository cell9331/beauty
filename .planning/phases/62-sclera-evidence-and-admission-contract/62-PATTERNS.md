## Evidence Data Flow

```text
ignored licensed originals
  -> isolated guarded derivative harness
  -> ignored original/mask/after + local fixed review
  -> 62 private runner (path only in child memory)
  -> 62 adapter (actual digests + opaque rights projection)
  -> Phase 54 ReviewCore snapshot/reviews/reducer/serializer
  -> canonical aggregate-only ledger
```

The adapter must give each logical asset a unique basename such as
`sclera_fixture_001-original.png`; duplicate `original.png` basenames are
ambiguous to Phase 54 and must fail closed. Reviews are issued by ReviewCore,
not copied directly from an untrusted local file.

  `scleraRednessReduction`.
- Bundled preset files: exactly five, byte-for-byte unchanged, missing sclera

- Demo: exactly three disabled local-retouch taxonomy rows with nil mappings.
- Canonical decisions: teeth `2/2/2/0/2 open`, sclera
  `2/2/2/0/2 open` only after real review, upper eyelid exact closed.

## Landmines to Avoid

- Do not generalize the active teeth runner in a way that changes its discovery,
  privacy scan or already-verified ledger reproduction.
- Do not accept the legacy unguarded sclera mask; private derivatives use the
  guarded path and still require original-detail review.
- Do not hardcode experimental guard constants into production in this phase.
- Do not add the public field on a closed/malformed row, even defaulted to zero.
- Do not expose feature identity by changing the opaque admission carrier.
- Do not let a combined 0/1/2 count imply multiple Vision requests; the engine
  still owns one canonical request-local context.
- Do not rewrite preset JSON, add renderer cases, enable `eyes.redness`, or
  modify teeth provider/output behavior.
