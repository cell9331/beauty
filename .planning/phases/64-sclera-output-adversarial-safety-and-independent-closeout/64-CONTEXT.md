<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Public output matrix

- **D-01:** Add exactly one isolated case, `scleraRednessReduction_1p00`, using
  only `BeautyParameters(scleraRednessReduction: 1)` through `BeautySDK`.
- **D-02:** Renderer inventory advances from exactly 73 to 74. Existing IDs,
  61 stored fields, five presets, one facade processing call, and the existing
  presentation-free `--no-watermark` behavior remain unchanged.
- **D-03:** The private matrix stages one authorized redness positive, one
  authorized normal negative, and one deterministic no-face input. Baseline and
  active cases produce exactly six decoded PNGs.
- **D-04:** Private discovery, staging, generated output and review media stay
  ignored. No path, media metadata, digest, rights detail, support, mask, pixel,
  identity, or raw metric may become tracked or staged.

### Strict decoded acceptance

- **D-05:** Freeze a bounded no-follow PNG/mask decoder and mutation self-tests
  before accepting live output. Reject duplicates, stale files, symlinks,
  escapes, malformed/oversized images, dimension or alpha changes, incomplete
  inventories, and skipped evidence.
- **D-06:** The positive must have nonzero reviewed-sclera change and reduced
  red excess in at least one reviewed eye, bounded channel/luminance movement,
  retained texture, and zero reviewed-mask exterior changes. The normal
  negative stays within the Phase 63 naturalness bounds; no-face is byte exact.
- **D-07:** Reviewed masks are private post-render evaluation truth only. They
  cannot activate, suppress, steer, clip, or otherwise enter production.
- **D-08:** Decoded measurements corroborate the Phase 62 evidence decision and
  Phase 63 provider result; they do not replace admission authority.

### Adversarial protected-anatomy safety

- **D-09:** A color-independent oracle varies complete plausible aperture and
  pupil support over a bounded inward/outward/asymmetric grid while retaining
  independently authored truth for iris, pupil, highlights, lash/margin, skin,
  and aperture exterior. Candidate overlap with protected truth is always zero.
- **D-10:** A recolored-protected oracle makes iris/pupil/highlight and boundary
  pixels score-attractive, then exercises the real guard, score, feather,
  re-clip, transform, Q16 composition, and output path. Every protected and
  exterior RGBA byte remains identical.
- **D-11:** Synthetic challenge fixtures prove mechanics and safety only. They
  have zero evidence, admission, naturalness, or promotion weight.
- **D-12:** Blink/closure, gaze, glare, glasses/contact-like occlusion, collapsed
  contour, malformed or missing pupil, unsafe one-eye support, and recovery
  sequences fail locally without guessing, stale state, or peer suppression.

### Original-detail review

- **D-13:** After thresholds and code are frozen, generate a fresh opaque A/B
  set from public-facade positive and negative outputs and inspect every item at
  original detail before role reveal.
- **D-14:** Positive output must visibly reduce redness while preserving natural
  sclera color, vessel/detail variation, highlights, iris/pupil identity,
  eyelid edges and skin. The normal negative must remain natural and nearly
  unchanged. Any contradiction blocks closeout.
- **D-15:** Durable review evidence stores only opaque roles, fixed categorical
  judgments, reason codes, decisions and aggregates; no locator, raw metric,
  geometry, pixels, identity, or free-form review prose.
- **D-16:** No post-review tuning is accepted. Any production change requires a
  fresh strict run, adversarial matrices, review and full conjunction.

### Atomic promotion and sequence

- **D-17:** The checker has fail-closed pre-promotion and post-promotion modes.
  Default requires `祛红血丝` future; post mode permits only its exact owner
  delta and requires all Phase 64 artifacts final.
- **D-18:** Promotion requires nonzero focused tests, helper/checker self-tests,
  fresh required private output, both adversarial oracles, original-detail
  review, compatibility/privacy/active-source checks, full SwiftPM and Demo
  regressions, review/fix, security and zero open HIGH threats.
- **D-19:** Promotion changes exactly `眼睛 | 祛红血丝` to `implemented`, citing
  Phase 62 evidence/admission, Phase 63 provider/integration and Phase 64
  output/safety/review. Aggregate `眼睛` stays `partial` because `去脂` is future.
- **D-20:** `去脂` remains future and both eye local-retouch Demo rows remain
  disabled with nil mappings. No renderer or status credit is borrowed by it.
- **D-21:** Phase 65 cannot begin until an independent post-promotion rerun
  confirms SCLERA-14...18 and OUT-05, exact owner agreement, 8/8 HIGH closed,
  privacy/artifact cleanliness and no conditional or skipped evidence.

### Agent Discretion

The agent may choose helper decomposition, opaque private names, deterministic
challenge layouts and the smallest table-driven XCTest organization. It may not
weaken Phase 63 guards, zero protected/exterior identity, exact counts, private
evidence handling, or the pre/post promotion order.

</decisions>
