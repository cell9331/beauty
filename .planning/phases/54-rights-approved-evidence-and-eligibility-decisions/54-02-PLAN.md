- `constants`: deeply frozen feature, polarity, rights-status, evidence-role, review-reason, gate-reason, score, row-count, byte, asset-size, and decoded-dimension allowlists/bounds.

- `validateReview(snapshot, review)` and `rowPasses(snapshotRow, review)`: enforce exact fields, integer scores, reason consistency, and frozen positive/negative predicates.
- `evaluateFeature(snapshot, reviews, designQualification)`: returns one feature-local decision/reason/count record. - `buildDurableExport(featureInputs)`: builds the exact positive-allowlist object.
- `serializeDurableExport(featureInputs)`: returns stable two-space LF JSON with one final newline and no time/session/environment argument.
