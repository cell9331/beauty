## Implementation Decisions

### Exact Caps and Directionality
- Finalize `mouthYPosition`, `mouthTilt`, `mouthXPosition`, `lipPeakDefinition`, and `lipPlump` at the existing evidence-backed exact `0.25` caps; do not raise provisional values or change public `-1...1` / `0...1` normalization.
- Lock both directions for all three signed controls and positive-only behavior for the two local lip controls through resolver/provider assertions, including exact capped-count accounting for over-cap inputs.
- Preserve the shipped `mouthSize`, `mouthWidth`, and `smile` caps and semantics; Phase 40 proves the complete eight-field mouth geometry set without reinterpreting old controls.
- Diagnostics remain aggregate and redacted: no field names, raw landmark/control-point values, coordinates, paths, or image-derived payloads in warnings or metrics.

### Degradation and Freshness
- Missing outer-lip support removes all eight mouth geometry fields; missing inner-lip support removes only `lipPeakDefinition` and `lipPlump`, while whole-mouth transforms and shipped outer-lip controls remain eligible.
- Reused geometry scales every eligible non-eye mouth field exactly once by `0.5`; stale or no-face geometry removes dependent mouth work while color/filter and independent `lipColor` behavior continue under their existing policies.
- Provider-empty output is authoritative per field: unsupported fields are removed before totals, counts, warnings, metrics, and final dispatch; eligible siblings remain active.
- Transition tests must cover fresh-to-reused, reused-to-fresh, fresh-to-stale, stale-to-fresh, and face-to-no-face behavior without retained stale mouth work.

### Combined Conflict Convergence
- Use one provider-eligible retained baseline for combined face, eye, six-field nose, and eight-field mouth work; do not introduce a second weakening path or field-specific conflict diagnostic.
- Each active retained field is weakened at most once per resolve, preserving signed direction and exact effective-strength/emission agreement.
- Convergence may remove at most fourteen nose/mouth fields (six nose plus eight mouth); each removal must be monotonic, and unsupported fields must not contribute to totals, weakened counts, scales, warnings, metrics, or dispatch.
- Freeze exact mixed-domain regressions for retained totals, counts, scale, warning presence/absence, safe sibling continuation, and provider output equality.

### Boundaries and Promotion
- Active-source scans fail closed on public lip-support types, raw geometry leakage, internal imports from Demo/renderer, network/cloud/commercial paths, new dependencies, compatibility inventory drift, unclassified source matches, and tracked/generated artifacts.
- Promotion is atomic and evidence-led: update the blueprint ledger and every current owner only after focused/full tests, security, validation, review, and boundary gates pass.
- Mark exactly `上下`, `倾斜`, `左右`, `M唇`, and true `丰唇` implemented; keep `白牙` future and branch-level `嘴唇` partial.
