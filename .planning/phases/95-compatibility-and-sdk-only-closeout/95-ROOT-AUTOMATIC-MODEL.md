## Source-visible transition model

Exact Q8 luminance, original16 row bins and original source-owned eye exclusions.
For each admitted half row, ordered exterior-to-center, use full exterior affine
background plus short interior affine transition support. All observed samples
are bounded by2 luminance units; supported interior lengths4/8/16/32 are fixed
before private source inspection. Fit lines by exact rational polygon clipping.
Background intercept is bounded by its first observed sample; slope bounds512
contain every adjacent-byte line compatible with the error box.

First retain the null explanation: a single affine line fitting the entire half
row. Such a row cannot supply paired structure. Otherwise enumerate every cut
between source samples with at least4 exterior and4 interior samples. For every
support length, keep all feasible outer/inner lines. Require their slope change
to have one strict sign over the whole feasible product and at least8 units of
contrast over the support's actual count-minus-one pixel span. Intersect their possible crossing interval
with that cut's closed source-pixel gap. Linear-fractional extrema occur at product
vertices because the denominator has a fixed nonzero sign. This encloses feasible
subpixel knots instead of choosing a maximum gradient or best fit.

Merge overlapping same-sign intervals only. Pair equal inward polarities (opposite
image-X polarities) across the original midpoint; retain every pair. At least12
source-only rows remain required. A layered graph retains forward/backward
reachable explanations under the existing4px continuity bound, avoiding an
exponential beam search. More than64 nodes per row rejects rather than truncates.
A120-second cooperative budget bounds the Python model, in addition to the
existing180-second Swift extraction budget. Exhaustion is unavailable.
The current graph is a conservative pairwise envelope, not a proof of one jointly
consistent continuous trajectory. Future measurement must keep that overapproximation
or solve joint consistency; it must not choose one favorable trajectory.

This model covers a visible source transition, not arbitrary hidden anatomy.
Below-noise structures and unmodeled texture are not automatically identified.
Candidate counts alone cannot approve structure ownership. The diagnostic remains
explicitly non-crediting; source/neutral/sibling/protection predicates remain pending.

on their generated domain. The new helper enumerates all horizontal source cells,
intersects the3 RGB interpolation inequalities exactly, and keeps disconnected
feasible components. No gain/offset/blur variables are introduced: the canonical

is passed as measured motion truth.

Two additional native tests join actual canonical pixels to this correspondence
and the existing conservative forward localization: source-defined boundaries
must narrow on the existing positive and remain unchanged on the existing negative.
Both pass. They do not establish a universal byte-error bound or source ownership.
Before portrait scoring, the exact real-image canonicalization, root-only route,
source/neutral/three siblings and all metadata must be admitted. A vertical or
legacy-sampling sibling cannot silently use the horizontal model.

No source registration, measurement admission or COMPLETE is created.

Before --inspect-source, an independent reviewer supplies
95-ROOT-AUTOMATIC-MODEL-REVIEW.json with schema phase95-automatic-model-review-v1,

That approval permits the source-only diagnostic only. Retain all failures and
unknowns; subsequent model changes require new review, not overwritten history.

Failure diagnostics retain aggregate processed/null/paired-row and node counts.

identities, pixel samples or coordinates are exported by these counters.
