## Nonblocking Technical Debt

### Phase 64 downstream rerun mode

The canonical Phase 64 verdict and its SHA-bound Phase 65 consumer are valid,
but `check_phase64_sclera_closeout.py --terminal-final` still authenticates the
old candidate-era repository/source delta. It therefore reports `candidate
source manifest stale` after legitimate Phase 65 changes. Add a future
post-downstream mode that validates the canonical Phase 64 artifact/hash without
requiring pre-Phase-65 owner bytes.

Affected requirements: SCLERA-14 through SCLERA-18, OUT-05, OUT-07, OUT-09.

### Lifecycle presentation density

`PLANS.md` intentionally preserves completed, failed, requarantined and final
Phase 64 records under its current Active ledger. The exact canonical authority
is unambiguous when following `64-VERIFICATION.md`, but the presentation is
dense and could be normalized in a later planning-ledger maintenance change.

Affected requirements: SCLERA-18, OUT-07, OUT-09.
