# Phase 62: Sclera Evidence and Admission Contract - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-08-07
**Status:** Ready for planning
**Mode:** Autonomous recommendations accepted under the active `--auto` run

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Establish one independent rights-approved `sclera_redness` evidence decision
and, only if that exact decision opens, append one compatibility-safe positive-
only `scleraRednessReduction` SDK intent plus one request-local opaque demand.
The phase may prepare private ignored mask/after derivatives and run local
structured review, but it does not implement a production per-eye provider,
guard, transform, renderer case, Demo mapping, realtime path, model, or product
promotion. A missing or failed genuine pair keeps sclera exactly absent while
the completed teeth slice remains unchanged.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Independent genuine sclera evidence

- **D-01:** The feature identity is exactly `sclera_redness`. A positive must be
  a genuine image with visible scleral redness/bloodshot appearance; a negative
  must be a genuine normal or already-low-redness sclera control. Teeth,
  mechanics, AI-generated, authorization-only, historical, upper-eyelid, and
  sibling rows contribute zero sclera product or admission weight.
- **D-02:** Positive and negative do not need to show the same person. Each must
  have its own opaque fixture ID, predeclared polarity/target, approved internal
  evaluation rights, and an exact-bound original/mask/after triple before it can
  become eligible.
- **D-03:** The user-provided minimum is two licensed original images plus their
  internal-evaluation authorization: one obvious redness positive and one normal
  negative. The repository's isolated local harness may create disposable
  ignored mask/after derivatives; generated derivatives never expand rights or
  count without the authorized originals.
- **D-04:** Current local inventory contains zero sclera fixtures and the
  canonical row remains closed for `missing_genuine_positive` and
  `missing_genuine_negative`. That state is a blocking intake fact, not
  permission to fabricate evidence, reuse the teeth pair, or add an inert
  production field.

### Frozen review and admission criteria

- **D-05:** Freeze criteria before opening images: positive target presence and
  bounded redness reduction; negative stability; useful sclera coverage; zero
  iris, pupil, highlight, lash, skin, makeup, or aperture-exterior leakage;
  preserved vessel/detail variation, luminance, structure, and natural color.
- **D-06:** Review remains local, blinded, and original-detail. It records only
  target presence, mask coverage 1...5, protected leakage, naturalness 1...5,
  structure change, accept/reject, and a fixed reason code. Criteria or
  thresholds cannot be tuned after outcome inspection.
- **D-07:** Both selected fixtures must pass rights, asset binding, review, and
  acceptance. The canonical open row is exactly two eligible, two reviewed, two
  accepted, zero rejected, and naturalness weight two; any rejected, missing,
  malformed, ambiguous, substituted, or incomplete row keeps sclera closed.
- **D-08:** The Phase 54 ReviewCore, trusted authorization binding, reducer, and
  serializer remain the sole decision authority. Sclera is appended to the
  existing canonical ledger without changing the already-open teeth row or the
  closed upper-eyelid row.

### Compatibility-safe sclera intent

- **D-09:** Only an independently open canonical sclera decision may append
  `scleraRednessReduction`. It is one positive-only `Float`, finite-normalized
  to `0...1`, defaulting to `0`; non-finite input becomes zero and finite out-of-
  range input clamps.
- **D-10:** Append the field at the current model tail in stored-property,
  `CodingKeys`, initializer, encode/decode, equality, and reflection order. The
  resulting exact inventory is 61 fields: 60 numeric values plus optional
  `filterId`. Existing labeled source calls and missing-key legacy payloads
  remain neutral.
- **D-11:** Preserve all five bundled preset files byte-for-byte and decode the
  missing sclera key as zero. Renderer inventory remains exactly 73 and this
  phase adds no sclera renderer/output case. The Demo retains exactly three
  disabled local-retouch rows with nil mappings.
- **D-12:** The public/Codable surface exposes only scalar intent. Eye contours,
  pupils, highlights, masks, candidate colors, vessel-like detail, fixture
  identity, rights/review state, and decision internals remain private,
  request-local or local-only, and non-persistent.

### Sclera-only demand and failure isolation

- **D-13:** `BeautyEffectResolver.localRetouchAdmission(parameters:)` remains
  the sole production admission authority. A direct normalized positive sclera
  value creates exactly one feature-neutral opaque demand in addition to any
  independently requested teeth demand; zero/missing/normalized-away sclera
  creates none.
- **D-14:** Teeth intent, global whitening/brightness/color, skin effects, eye
  geometry, Testing hooks, `去脂` proxies, aliases, Demo IDs, and Chinese titles
  cannot activate sclera demand. Conversely, sclera intent cannot activate,
  suppress, multiply, or alter teeth demand.
- **D-15:** Valid-invalid-valid, teeth-only, sclera-only, both-intent, legacy,
  no-face, pixel-buffer, and reset contracts retain request-local isolation. At
  this phase, demand proves intent admission only; no production eye support,
  mask, provider, transform, or output is implied.
- **D-16:** A closed or malformed sclera evidence row produces exact sclera
  absence without rolling back the 60-field teeth model, one teeth provider,
  73 renderer cases, implemented `白牙`/`嘴唇`, or current full regressions.

### Privacy, safety, and lifecycle

- **D-17:** Private discovery is NUL-safe, bounded, nofollow, exact-feature and
  exact-polarity. Paths and rights detail exist only in child-process memory;
  durable exports contain opaque IDs, fixed judgments/reasons, decisions, and
  aggregate counts. No media, locator, digest, reviewer identity, geometry,
  pixel, raw metric, scanner match, error payload, or freeform prose is tracked.
- **D-18:** Every evidence, compatibility, alias, sibling, privacy, and lifecycle
  threat is blocking HIGH under OWASP ASVS Level 1. Phase 63 remains blocked
  unless canonical Phase 62 verification records an independently open sclera
  row, scalar/demand compatibility, zero open HIGH, and no borrowed evidence.

### the agent's Discretion

The agent may choose filenames, helper decomposition, deterministic mutation
fixtures, and atomic plan boundaries. It may not weaken the exact evidence
pair, Phase 54 authority, privacy allowlist, field/demand isolation, Demo
absence, or downstream phase boundary.

</decisions>

## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets

- Phase 59 already adapts a private ignored genuine bundle into the exact Phase
  54 ReviewCore/serializer contract without tracking locators or rights detail.
  Phase 62 should generalize or parallel that adapter narrowly for sclera while
  preserving the canonical teeth row byte-for-byte.
- The Phase 54 trusted authorization registry, structured reviewer, reducer,
  durable export allowlist, and mutation harness already implement the correct
  evidence authority and privacy boundary.
- `BeautyParameters` and resolver tests already prove one trailing local-
  retouch scalar and one demand; the sclera contract should extend those exact
  inventories rather than create a second admission API.

### Established Patterns

- Evidence gates fail closed and are independent. Mechanics can corroborate
  mask/transform behavior but cannot supply product, naturalness, or admission
  weight.
- Raw portrait-derived support and media stay ignored/request-local. Durable
  evidence uses fixed structured rows and aggregate-only output.
- Compatibility changes are append-only; bundled neutral presets remain source-
  stable and missing legacy keys decode as zero.

### Integration Points

- The private evidence adapter feeds Phase 54 ReviewCore and the canonical
  `54-EVIDENCE-DECISIONS.json` owner.
- After and only after an open decision, the new scalar connects the current
  parameter tail to `BeautyEffectResolver.localRetouchAdmission` as one opaque
  sclera demand. Provider work remains Phase 63.
- Demo tests remain negative owners for `eyes.redness`; product ledgers remain
  future/partial until Phase 64 output and promotion, not Phase 62 admission.

## Specific Ideas

- The prior teeth evidence flow is the implementation template, but every
  feature ID, fixture row, review, decision, and aggregate must be independent.

  genuine imagery, clear polarity, exact assets, frozen review, and protected-
  anatomy safety.

  requirement explicit and stop before scalar admission if the pair is absent.
