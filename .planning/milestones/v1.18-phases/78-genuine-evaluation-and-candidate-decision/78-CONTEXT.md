# Phase 78 Context

## Objective

Evaluate the Phase 77 deterministic editor under the frozen Phase 75 genuine-
evidence and blinded-review contract, admit an optional additive-map comparator
only when its rights and safety provenance are complete, and produce one
privacy-safe recommendation for Phase 79.

## Locked decisions

- The Phase 75 evaluator is the admission authority. Phase 78 cannot weaken
  its category, rights, hash, privacy, or metadata-only mechanics rules.
- A generated or metadata-only manifest proves evaluator mechanics only. It
  must produce `mechanics-only-not-promotion`, never a genuine efficacy pass.
- A missing, incomplete, malformed, or rights-invalid bundle fails closed with
  normalized reasons and exact public absence. No tuning or candidate ranking
  is permitted from substitute fixtures.
- The deterministic Phase 77 editor is the baseline mechanics candidate. An
  optional learned comparator is absent unless model, data, and redistribution
  rights plus bounded additive-map and all-safety-gate evidence are supplied.
- Durable Phase 78 output contains only hashes, opaque fixture IDs, aggregate
  counts/metrics, normalized reasons, candidate dispositions, and one decision.
  Raw pixels, masks, landmarks, paths, review prose, and private locators stay
  outside the repository and process output.
- No public field, preset, renderer case, resource, provider, route, or SPI is
  added in this phase. Phase 79 consumes the recommendation and preserves the
  exact 61/5/74 surface on failure.

## Evidence state at phase start

No rights-approved private genuine bundle is present in the workspace. The
expected reproducible result is therefore a mechanics-only, non-promotion
recommendation while the typed missing-bundle path remains testable.

## Out of scope

Physical-device, commercial, packaging, shipping, launch, release-readiness,
and genuine visual-quality claims remain outside this SDK-owned phase.
