<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Risk | Mitigation |
|----------|------|------------|
| Vision observation → semantic owner | Landmarks silently become fullness semantics | Provider-owned approval is mandatory and landmark-only inputs fail closed |
| Left eye → right eye | Shared confidence/mask permits unsupported-eye edits | Independent side state and source-exact fallback |
| Support mask → later composition | Non-finite/out-of-bounds or overlapping ownership leaks edits | Finite hard containment, unique indices, overlap-to-source outcome |
| Request-local support → diagnostics | Raw masks/landmarks leak to persistent output | Non-Codable types and aggregate-only custom reflection |
</threat_model>
