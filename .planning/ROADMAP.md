# Roadmap: Beauty

## Overview

Beauty v1.0 shipped a modular local-first iOS beauty SDK plus a rich SwiftUI Demo validation app. Full phase detail is archived in `.planning/milestones/v1.0-ROADMAP.md`; this living roadmap now stays small and milestone-oriented.

Advanced makeup, segmentation, body shaping, stickers, AI style, video export, commercial packaging, and distribution-grade release hardening remain future milestone scope.

## Milestones

- **v1.0 MVP** - Phases 1-7, shipped 2026-06-23. See `.planning/milestones/v1.0-ROADMAP.md`.
- **Next milestone** - Not defined yet. Start with `$gsd-new-milestone`.

## Backlog

No active next-milestone phases are defined yet.

Candidate directions to consider during `$gsd-new-milestone`:

- Release hardening: real-device camera/Vision QA, performance budgets, long-run memory/thermal checks, and production render regression.
- Advanced beauty modules: makeup templates/components, skin repair, eyebrows/teeth/hairline/forehead, and multi-face support.
- Creative modules: segmentation/background, body shaping, stickers, AR masks, style effects, and video export.
- Distribution readiness: privacy manifest, resource-pack strategy, binary packaging, compatibility matrix, and commercial SDK documentation.
