## Measurement boundary

A valid endpoint pair can constrain left-to-right correspondence without establishing a neutral-to-endpoint strength or response at intermediate positions. Do not divide an endpoint span by two to manufacture neutral strengths. The single 去脂 endpoint requires a separately qualified reference before its effect can be isolated.

Each feature report must state whether its comparison is a direct endpoint pair, a source-to-endpoint comparison with a qualified source, or an unresolved state comparison. Stable-region alignment, independent correspondences, accepted/rejected counts, and protected-region observations are measurement tasks beyond this inventory.

Contact sheets, exact viewport coordinates, per-file hashes, and the detailed machine-readable audit remain in the ignored local measurement directory. This aggregate inventory contains no landmark coordinates, masks, dense fields, or private absolute paths. This input audit does not imply that any effect has been reproduced or that an SDK, simulator, or device validation has run.
