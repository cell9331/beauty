**Status: localized upward motion identified; candidate field proposed, not implemented.** Analysis dated 2026-10-16 compares `IMG_1943.PNG → IMG_1944.PNG`, the observed left-to-right slider span. Neutral strength, symmetric endpoints, and intermediate response are not established.

The changed region is concentrated around each outer eye, with upward dy and much smaller dx. Stationary texture distributed across the center and inner region supports a spatially localized effect and argues against uniform whole-eye translation. A rigid eye-core rotation about a pivot near the inner corner remains a competing hypothesis: the present analysis does not include the same-core rigid fit or pairwise-distance test needed to exclude it. Independent source-texture checks support the direction and substantial motion; weakly textured transition boundaries remain uncertain.
