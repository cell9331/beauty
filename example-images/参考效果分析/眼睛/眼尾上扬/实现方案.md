# Outer-eye lift: compact upward-field candidate

**Status: proposed model, not implemented or visually qualified.** The [evidence chain](分析链路.md) identifies a local upward effect and records uncertain transitions. Common coordinate, composition, full-weight derivative, inverse, and original-image sampling rules are in [implementation conventions](../实现公共约定.md).

## Minimal source geometry and field

Freeze source eye width `L > 0`, outer-corner neighborhood, tangent `e_t` from inner to outer corner, and inferior orthogonal axis `e_d` with a positive image-y component. With dimensionless coordinates `t=(p−c)·e_t/L` and `n=(p−c)·e_d/L`, propose:

```text
B(z) = (1−z²)^3 for |z| < 1, otherwise 0
H(t) = B((t−t_c)/sigma_t)
K(n) = B((n−n_c)/sigma_n)
w(p) = P(p) H(t) K(n)
F_s(p) = p + s a w(p) e_d,       0 <= s <= 1, a < 0
```

`s` parameterizes a proposed map from the observed left endpoint to the observed right endpoint. Its zero is that source image, not an inferred neutral. `a` is a pixel amplitude for this source geometry. The envelope is a design hypothesis; the reference does not establish this polynomial, its precise center, symmetric widths, or a neutral response law.

The center is near the changing outer-eye region, not a mandated fixed corner point. The support includes moving lid tissue and neighboring skin and decays inward before a separately observed protected inner region. `P` encodes continuous protection and any additional support limits; a binary semantic eye mask is insufficient because some surrounding skin follows the motion. Different inner/outer or superior/inferior transition widths should only be added if independent boundary observations require them.

## Complete Jacobian

For fixed per-image geometry, let `v=a e_d`. Then:

```text
grad_H = B'((t−t_c)/sigma_t) e_t/(L sigma_t)
grad_K = B'((n−n_c)/sigma_n) e_d/(L sigma_n)
grad_w = H K grad_P + P K grad_H + P H grad_K
J_F = I + s v (grad_w)^T
det(J_F) = 1 + s (grad_w · v)
```

Check the whole derivative, including the protection factor and both transitions. A narrow return to stationary skin may compress even when the lifted core is a safe translation. Apply the shared full-domain injectivity and inverse-residual rules to the actual combined field. A sufficient conservative contraction bound is `s ||v|| sup ||grad_w|| < 1`; this must be demonstrated for the proposed geometry and amplitude. It is not inferred from positive determinant at a few sampled points.

The inferior bump is a low-degree-of-freedom candidate, not a demonstrated rejection of rigid core rotation. A rigid eye-core rotation about a pivot near the inner corner can retain nearly stationary inner texture while lifting the outer region and remains a competing model, alongside a locally weighted rotation. Distinguishing them requires full dx/dy and pairwise-distance comparisons on the same source-defined core correspondences; that comparison has not been completed for this feature. If independently supported residuals require a small tangent component in `v`, compare that alternative as well. Any added model must explain independent correspondence rather than merely improve dense-flow fitting, and its complete derivatives must be included.

## Calibration, protection, and rejection

Use the observed pair to fit upward amplitude and spatial decay jointly, with moving and stationary correspondences scored separately. The sampled −20.67 px motion and region percentiles are reference observables, not a production constant or a prescribed amplitude. A new photo requires its own eye width and geometry; no normalized coefficient is supplied without that measurement. Intermediate `s` interpolation is a proposal and needs additional references for qualification.

Protection should explicitly include inner-eye texture, the central iris observation, brow, bridge, and distant cheek while allowing the demonstrated moving outer lid. Reject unreliable geometry, invalid supports, an unsafe complete field, or a failed inverse solve according to the common identity/failure rule. Sampling the original once under the solved inverse is required; repeatedly warping the preceding output or blending misregistered photos would obscure a wrong footprint.

Acceptance requires that the actual output raise the outer region by the independently supported amount, preserve the observed inner texture, reproduce the dy decay, and remain within the horizontal correspondence uncertainty. Examine both upper and lower lid contours and surrounding skin. A correct sign, safe Jacobian, successful call chain, or changed pixels does not prove this effect. The current analysis provides a candidate and testable rejection criteria only.
