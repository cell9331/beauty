# Eye height: candidate field

**Status: proposed mathematics only.** This does not identify proprietary internals, implement the SDK, or qualify reproduced appearance. Read the [evidence chain](分析链路.md) and [shared definitions, derivatives, safety and sampling](../实现公共约定.md).

## Minimum geometry and source domain

Test a normal-direction expansion about a fitted source anchor line, with independently fitted surrounding support. A pure normal model is the minimum candidate; retain horizontal residuals as a challenge, rather than declaring them zero. Freeze reliable source eye geometry: center/reference c, positive scale L, nasal-to-temporal tangent e_t and inferior normal e_n. The common definitions provide t,n and all normalization derivatives. For this pair s=0 is the observed left-endpoint image and s=1 the right-endpoint mapping, not a neutral-to-positive control. Set the candidate response to zero at s=0 and fit its endpoint value; choosing a linear response between them would be an unmeasured design choice.

## Forward map and full Jacobian

```text
r(p,s) = L a(s) (n(p)−n_0) e_n
D(p,s) = w(p) r(p,s)
J_r = a(s) e_n e_n^T
J_F = I + w a(s) e_n e_n^T + r (grad_w)^T
```

Use the common compact support/protection product and its complete derivative. Smoothstep is only one possible design kernel. The measured optical-flow window and a binary eye/iris mask do not define w. Fit any superior/inferior or nasal/temporal asymmetry from the source-matched transition, not from the control name. If geometry or directions vary spatially rather than remaining frozen constants, their derivatives also enter J_r.

## Calibration, scope and protection

n_0 is a fitted source-local anchor, not an assumed iris-center line. Choose one source center/basis convention so an extra free translation does not make the anchor unidentifiable. Fit a(s=1) only after matching sensitivity is acceptable; current relative gains are approximate diagnostics. Allow independent superior/inferior envelope widths before adding independent core gains. A repeatable horizontal residual could require a small tangential term; the present pure-normal candidate must be rejected if it cannot match that residual.

The visible iris and surrounding lid/scleral texture can follow the opening change. Protect canthal neighborhoods according to measured decay rather than imposing exact zeros from the feature name. Keep brows/bridge/remote cheek texture independently stationary and preserve realistic source coverage at newly exposed lid regions; the field cannot invent disoccluded texture.

The anchor line, superior/inferior gain split, horizontal follow motion and support boundaries remain uncertain. Dense core DIS–Farneback P95 differences reach 6.62/7.27 px and forward/backward P95 1.20/0.98 px. Do not promote the fitted 1.5-scale estimate or a visually chosen fixed corner into calibration. Capture a neutral and intermediate states, then add independent upper/lower lid and iris correspondences.

## Future evaluation sequence

1. Admit reliable source geometry and ownership; retain identity on degenerate or uncertain inputs.
2. Fit the internal model with independent moving correspondences, then determine support using separate decay/protection observations. Preserve accepted outliers and residual structure.
3. Evaluate the complete Jacobian and any multi-effect overlap; establish a valid inverse/coverage strategy. A safe core or positive sampled determinant alone is insufficient.
4. Solve q=F_s(p), check the inverse residual, and sample the original source once. Use the common explicit identity/failure result for unsafe support, failed inverse or uncovered targets.
5. Compare output contours, texture and protected regions against the actual endpoint mapping on the same source points.

Require upper/lower sign opposition, dominant normal expansion, correct aperture change and acceptable horizontal residual at independent texture points. A candidate that merely increases an abstract eye-height parameter or passes Jacobian checks has not reproduced this reference. Current dense-method sensitivity is an explicit calibration limit.

The zero/translation/expansion/rotation controls reported at the [eye index](../README.md) qualify the measuring method only on their own synthetic cases. No actual candidate output has been rendered or accepted in this task.
