## Current behavior and change decision

The map implements dominant normal expansion with a contour-derived anchor, quartic lateral cutoff, saturation and iris-interior attenuation. The reference's narrow lid transitions are method-sensitive.

Test the anchor, envelope and interior attenuation as separate hypotheses. Retain normal expansion; avoid fitting a dense peak that has not earned independent precision.

Current change location: [BeautyContinuousEyeMap.swift](../../../../BeautySDK/Sources/BeautyEffects/Render/BeautyContinuousEyeMap.swift#L610); inspect only the term and shared helpers actually affected.

## Ordered tasks

1. Establish a C0 baseline for upper/lower signed motion, iris/sclera following, canthal transitions and core affine residual. Retain mobile disagreements rather than selecting the best-looking optical-flow curve.
2. Fit the anchor and source scale with independent texture before testing symmetric versus asymmetric gains/falloff. Use the minimum parameter set; do not assume the existing .52-span cutoff is either measured or wrong.
3. Compare current attenuation with a controlled unattenuated candidate at the same admitted geometry and support. Modify only a residual supported by held-out iris/lid texture, then recheck the full transition derivative and inverse.

Use the shared C0 comparator before fitting; C1 field/inverse rules and C2 ownership/composition apply only to changes actually selected. Do not silently change centered slider, serialized scalar or unavailable-host semantics. A field-coefficient change must be evaluated through the actual admitted forward/relative map, including pose and safety scaling.
