# Lower-lid lowering: falsifiable candidate field

**Status: proposed mathematical model only; incomplete calibration, no SDK implementation or reproduction qualification.** Read the [evidence limitations](分析链路.md) first. Coordinate, full-weight derivative, inverse-sampling, composition, and failure rules are shared in [implementation conventions](../实现公共约定.md).

## Geometry and forward map

Freeze source-image geometry for one field evaluation: eye width `L > 0`, tangent `e_t` from inner to outer corner, orthogonal inferior direction `e_d` with a positive image-y component, and a source lower-lid curve `n = h(t)`. Define dimensionless local coordinates `t = (p−c)·e_t/L`, `n = (p−c)·e_d/L`. The curve and supports must be estimated on each new source; this sample's coordinates and widths are not constants.

Let `B(z) = (1−z²)^3` for `|z| < 1`, and zero otherwise. It is a proposed compact C2 envelope. Separate along-lid location from distance to the lower contour:

```text
H(t) = B((t−t0)/sigma_t)
z(p) = (n−h(t))/sigma_n
K(p) = B(z(p))
w(p) = P(p) H(t) K(p)
F_s(p) = p + s w(p) v,       0 <= s <= 1
v = a_d e_d                 [minimal vertical candidate]
v = a_t e_t + a_d e_d       [conditional vector-bump alternative]
```

`s=0` denotes the observed left endpoint source; `s=1` is a proposed mapping to the observed right endpoint. Neither is asserted to be a neutral photo. `a_d > 0` gives inferior motion; `a_t` is unresolved. `a_d` and `a_t` are pixel amplitudes for this geometry. Normalizing them by a measured `L` would be a scale-transfer design choice; no numerical normalized coefficient is established here.

`t0` lies in the outer lower-lid region, with widths fitted from independently supported motion and decay. `P` is a continuous protection/support factor, not a hard semantic eye cutout. The lower eyelid and immediately following skin may move; brow, inner corner, bridge, and distant cheek provide separate constraints. A protected iris interior must not be assumed to include every ambiguous boundary pixel.

## Full derivative and safe rendering

For frozen source geometry and constant `v`, the complete Jacobian is:

```text
grad_t = e_t/L; grad_n = e_d/L
grad_H = B'((t−t0)/sigma_t) grad_t/sigma_t
grad_z = (grad_n − h'(t) grad_t)/sigma_n
grad_K = B'(z) grad_z
grad_w = H K grad_P + P K grad_H + P H grad_K
J_F = I + s v (grad_w)^T
det(J_F) = 1 + s (grad_w · v)
```

The protection gradient and the lower-lid curve derivative are essential. A displacement clamp alone cannot prevent compression at a narrow transition. Use the shared full-domain derivative/injectivity checks, include any combined left/right supports, and solve the inverse map before sampling the original once. A sufficient conservative contraction budget is `s ||v|| sup ||grad_w|| < 1`; it is a design condition to verify over the actual support, not a measured property of this reference.

Unreliable lid geometry, missing scleral texture, conflicting correspondences, an unsafe transition, or an unsuccessful inverse solve must produce the shared identity/failure behavior. Widening a support into an independently protected region is not an acceptable way to conceal a safety failure.

## Calibration and acceptance boundary

Fit a downward amplitude and compact support first, retaining the dense field as an uncertain proposal. Do not set the amplitude to the estimated dense peak: the moving-point validation failed there. Keep the vector-bump alternative uncalibrated unless independent matches establish its horizontal term. An asymmetric envelope, a spatially rotating vector, or an iris reconstruction mechanism requires new evidence rather than automatic extra parameters.

Acceptance would require a correct lower-lid contour, independent moving texture at both the peak and decay, preserved inner/upper observations, agreement of both dx and dy, and the actual pixel result under positive and negative controls. A candidate can be rejected by a stable horizontal residual, incorrect motion near the iris edge, movement of protected texture, or an overbroad footprint even if its Jacobian and inverse are valid. The current material completes analysis and a reviewable hypothesis; it does not meet these reproduction criteria.
