## Question and input qualification

The question is whether the control extends the temporal end of the eye locally or changes the whole eye. The measured pair is `IMG_1949.PNG` → `IMG_1950.PNG`, observed left → right slider endpoint. The screenshots already share a photo grid. Original crop bytes are retained, without resampling or local deformable alignment. The pair does not establish a common neutral state or an intermediate response curve.

Before measurement, `D(p) = p_target − p_source` was defined with positive x right and positive y down, in screenshot pixels. External brow/nose/cheek texture is used for stability checks. Temporal corner, temporal sclera, iris interior, nasal sclera, and nasal corner are observed separately. UI and photo overlays are excluded. Native eye details were inspected alongside the signed fields and profiles.

## Direction and extent of the observed span

The temporal region of the image-left eye moves left, while that of the image-right eye moves right. The main response is horizontally outward. Small downward motion accompanies parts of the active region, but not with a perfectly uniform sign in every texture match. Most central-eye and nasal texture remains quiet.

The two methods preserve the main direction, while their support widths and small vertical predictions differ. A precise field kernel or boundary cannot be read from the smoothest curve.

## Independent correspondence checks

Primary source-only NCC accepts **273 of 386** candidates. It rejects **71 low-texture, 21 ambiguous, 4 reverse-ambiguous, and 17 edge** candidates. Search-boundary peaks receive a larger search before acceptance. No accepted-match rule uses consistency with the dense field.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |

| Stationary, measured magnitude < 0.5 px | 259 | 0.021 | 0.086 |

Three accepted points lie between classes. The stationary population must not dilute the moving-region difference.

The smaller-template eye-only source check accepts **106 of 127** candidates, rejecting **16 ambiguous, 3 reverse-ambiguous, 1 reverse-closure, and 1 low-texture**. Its **15 mobile** points have median/P95 difference **0.67/1.51 px** from DIS; **83 stationary** points have **0.034/0.140 px**. Eight lie between classes. The source-only selector and match-quality gates are independent of flow agreement, but nearby patches are correlated rather than independent image subjects.

The supplementary source-defined active-canthus windows contain **4/3 accepted points**. Their dx spans are **−11.41 to −5.86 px / +5.67 to +12.08 px**. Their dy spans are **−0.18 to +0.80 px / −0.10 to +1.13 px**. This directly supports outward motion and a smaller, nonuniform vertical component. These samples cannot prove a constant-displacement plateau or an exactly horizontal operation.

Temporal-sclera windows have **3/2 accepted points**, with dx **−6.92 to −0.31 px / +7.21 to +7.53 px**. The support extends into nearby sclera rather than affecting only a single corner anchor. Sparse sampling and dense-method differences leave the full transition shape unresolved.

## Protected observations

Both source-defined iris-interior windows are byte-identical. Their **5/3 supplementary matches** have displacement-magnitude P95 **0.170/0.041 px**. Both nasal-canthus windows and both nasal-sclera windows are also byte-identical. The nasal-canthus check has **3 points** on the image-left side, P95 **0.079 px**, and only **1 point** on the image-right side, magnitude **0.017 px**. The single point provides limited correspondence coverage; the unchanged pixel window is a separate observation on this pair.

Central-lid windows are mostly quiet but include small changed fringes, especially on the image-right lower lid. Therefore the whole lid contour should not be constrained to exact zero without checking its temporal transition.

External brow/nose/image-left cheek texture has NCC displacement-magnitude P95 **0.057/0.067/0.108 px**, from **58/30/13** points. The image-right cheek has just **2 points**. These checks support localization on this sample, not a claim of universal facial protection. Dense near-zero output in smooth skin is not independent proof.

## Fact → candidate → exclusions → unknowns

**Fact:** temporal horizontal movement is outward and localized, with quiet iris/nasal regions and a small vertical companion. **Candidate:** a compact local displacement centered at each temporal canthus, with a continuous fade through adjacent sclera and lid/skin. **Excluded as sufficient explanations:** whole-eye translation, uniform full-eye scaling, and rigid rotation of the entire eye, all of which would move observed quiet regions.

The [inner-canthus pair](../内眼角/分析链路.md) activates the nasal ends instead. This distinction supports separate active anchors and protection regions, rather than exchanging the sign of a full-eye length field.

**Unknown:** core plateau versus bump, exact support edge, whether the small dy is an eye-axis effect or a separate local term, endpoint-neutral decomposition, and intermediate response. More source-only correspondence through the temporal fade and a documented neutral capture would distinguish these choices. The [candidate scheme](实现方案.md) keeps amplitude, support, and the orthogonal component independently reviewable.
