# Outer-canthus candidate field

[Measured evidence](分析链路.md) · [Shared mathematics and rendering](../实现公共约定.md)

Status: **candidate only; not implemented or reference-validated**. This field is proposed to model the observed left-endpoint → right-endpoint span and does not identify the closed-source algorithm.

## Geometry and forward field

Obtain the source temporal-canthus anchor `c`, an eye-aligned horizontal basis, a downward local normal `e_v`, and source-derived eye scales. Let `e_t` point toward the temporal side, so its screen-horizontal component is negative for the image-left eye and positive for the image-right eye in this sample.

With `r = p − c`, `s = r·e_t`, and `t = r·e_v`, start with a compact local candidate:

```text
ρ²        = (s/R_s)² + (t/R_t)²
W(p)      = (max(0, 1 − ρ²))²
D_pair(p) = W(p) [A e_t + B e_v]
F_τ(p)    = p + τ D_pair(p),   0 ≤ τ ≤ 1
```

`A > 0` is chosen to model the observed outward component. `B` is to be fitted independently because accepted texture matches show a smaller vertical component and do not establish perfectly horizontal movement. A component arising from the chosen eye basis should be accounted for before adding a separate vertical term. The compact C1 bump is a design choice; the reference data do not distinguish it from every short-platform alternative.

`τ` is a proposed interpolation of this observed pair. It is not the measured UI slider response and has no established neutral at its midpoint. A neutral-based editor would require mappings from the same qualified source state, following the shared endpoint-domain convention.

## Calibration and active/protected regions

Fit the active field to the outward source-only matches: the sampled temporal corners span approximately **6–12 px** horizontally on this screenshot pair. Dense temporal-window P95 magnitudes **11.90/12.51 px** are complementary, method-sensitive summaries. Neither supplies a universal displacement budget, a fixed support radius, or a neutral-to-endpoint amplitude.

Fit each eye separately. Include temporal sclera and the observed temporal lid/skin fringe in the support, then continuously fade before the quiet iris interior and nasal side. Do not restrict the operation to a binary corner mask or move the entire eye as a rigid patch. If a smooth protection factor is used, constrain it with the byte-identical iris/nasal windows and independent correspondence, while retaining transition uncertainty at the lid fringe.

Eye-normalized scales must come from measured geometry and be checked on additional inputs. Exact bilateral symmetry and a fixed relation between horizontal/vertical amplitude remain design assumptions. Do not use the inner-canthus amplitude or support by sign reversal alone: its active location and neighboring geometry differ.

## Field safety and inverse sampling

For a locally constant vector `v = A e_t + B e_v`, `∇D = v ∇Wᵀ`; any protection factor adds its full derivative. The compact boundary's zero first derivative provides a smooth connection, but safety inside the fade still depends on amplitude relative to support width. Check the complete Jacobian, global injectivity, and inverse residual under the shared conventions.

Construct one forward field from the qualified original geometry, solve the target-to-source inverse, and sample the original pixels once. Do not repeatedly warp already edited images. If support conflicts with protected iris/nasal regions, the source geometry is uncertain, or the inverse cannot be verified, reduce or omit the candidate effect.

## Verification that could reject this model

Require correct signed temporal motion, the observed small dy behavior, quiet iris/nasal texture, and continuous fade through temporal sclera. Score the active points separately from stationary points, and retain all rejected or disagreeing matches. A broad interior residual would reject the chosen protection; a verified constant core would motivate a plateau; systematic dy residuals would challenge the local basis or `B`.

Continuity or a positive Jacobian does not prove the reference appearance. The current several-pixel dense-method sensitivity at transitions is a qualification limit, and a reproduction claim requires independent rendered evidence after a better-constrained target is available.
