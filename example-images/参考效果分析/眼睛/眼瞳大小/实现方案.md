# Iris-size candidate field

[Measured evidence](分析链路.md) · [Shared field, inverse, and sampling conventions](../实现公共约定.md)

Status: **general mathematical hypothesis; no reference-qualified calibration**. The model is proposed to approximate the observed left-endpoint → right-endpoint span. It is not a claim about the closed-source application's internal algorithm.

**2026-10-17 follow-up:** the [implementation record](实施与验证.md) identifies the explicit H2 polynomial instantiation, its derivative/inverse proof, directed tests and actual photo replay. That research implementation does not validate this general model or establish production parameters; the effect gate remains unpassed. The earlier H1 alternative and its failed fits are retained separately.

## Minimum geometry and forward model

For each eye, obtain a reliable iris center `c`, an eye-aligned horizontal unit vector `e_h`, its downward normal `e_v`, and source-derived horizontal/vertical scales `R_h, R_v`. The visible iris may be occluded; its fitted center and scale must carry uncertainty. Do not infer a complete iris boundary by treating the lid margin as iris texture.

Write `p − c = s e_h + t e_v`, and define `ρ² = (s/R_h)² + (t/R_v)²`. A low-complexity initial candidate is:

```text
D_pair(p) = G(ρ) [a s e_h + b t e_v]
F_τ(p)    = p + τ D_pair(p),   0 ≤ τ ≤ 1
```

Here `a > 0` and `b > 0` express local horizontal and vertical expansion. `G` has a possible central core and a continuous compact falloff; its core extent and boundary must be fitted rather than read from a feature name. `τ` is an interpolation between the two observed images, chosen for this candidate. It is not a measured slider response or a neutral-centered parameter.

An anisotropic support is allowed because eyelid occlusion and the observed displacement amplitudes do not establish a circular, isotropic operation. Start with one center and two gains per eye, plus the smallest support description that the independent matches can constrain. Do not add individual control points to absorb uncertain dense peaks.

## Calibration and protection

Fit the model to the same source points as the observed field, with mobile and stationary correspondences reported separately. The measured eye-window ranges, roughly **±10 px horizontally** and **±4.5–4.8 px vertically** at P1/P99, constrain an endpoint span on this screenshot scale. They do not identify `a`, `b`, or a neutral strength without measured source geometry.

The support must include the observed central lid and scleral follow motion. An iris segmentation multiplied directly into the field would create the wrong spatial behavior and potentially a sharp boundary. Fit the fade using texture correspondence around these structures. Give the outer canthus a quiet observation region; give the nasal canthus an allowed measured transition rather than forcing every nasal point to zero. Brow, nose, and cheek protection belongs outside this local support.

Exact upper/lower symmetry, equal gains in both eyes, and a linear slider are optional design assumptions, not established facts. A later neutral-based editor would need two mappings from the same neutral source and compare them on corresponding points; it cannot use half of this observed pair span as measured neutral strength.

## Continuity and rendering

Apply the complete spatial derivative, including the falloff and any protection weights. In a fixed local basis, if `A = a e_h e_hᵀ + b e_v e_vᵀ`, the displacement derivative contains both `G A` and `(A(p−c)) ∇Gᵀ`. Checking `a` and `b` alone misses the compression created by the outer fade.

Follow the shared requirements for full Jacobian checks, global injectivity, inverse-map residual, and a single final sample from the original pixels. If iris geometry is unreliable, support collides with a protected region, or an inverse fails, reduce or omit this candidate effect rather than synthesize a plausible-looking dense correspondence.

## Falsifiable validation

The candidate must reproduce both signed lobes and the measured lid/sclera motion while keeping outer-canthus and external texture quiet. Evaluate mobile correspondences, central iris texture, upper/lower margins, nasal fade, and temporal fade separately. Retain outliers and rejected-match reasons. Compare the proposed field with both dense methods and source-only NCC without filtering the latter by model agreement.

A systematic ring-only residual would challenge the linear-core choice; stable central lids in a new qualified reference would challenge the current support model; unexplained appearance/disocclusion changes would require narrowing the geometric claim. Existing cross-method differences of several pixels at transitions are a limit on target qualification, not an acceptable future reproduction error by definition.
