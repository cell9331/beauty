## Question and input qualification

The question is whether the observed control expands an iris alone, the full eye, or an intermediate region including adjacent structures. The comparison is `IMG_1939.PNG` → `IMG_1940.PNG`, observed left → right slider endpoint. The pair shares the screenshot photo grid; the analysis preserves the original crop bytes and adds no resampling or local alignment. This pair does not establish a common neutral state, symmetric endpoint strengths, or intermediate slider response.

Before measuring, the source-to-target convention was fixed as `D(p) = p_target − p_source`, with positive x right and positive y down, in screenshot pixels. Stable brow, nose, and cheek texture are external checks; both iris regions, lids, sclera, and canthi are observed separately. The local analysis excludes the UI and photo overlays. The native source/target details were inspected in addition to the overview figure.

## Observed spatial behavior

The signed horizontal field has opposing lobes at the two sides of each iris. The vertical field has an upward upper lobe and a downward lower lobe. The center is quieter than the margins. Motion falls away well before most of the outer canthal region. The field is not a constant translation, and a single uniform transformation of the whole eye would move structures that remain nearly stationary here.

## Independent correspondences and sensitivity

The primary NCC check selects source texture spatially, without initializing or accepting matches from the dense flow. It accepts **262 of 385** candidates. Rejections are 74 low-texture, 29 ambiguous, 2 low-NCC, 17 search/template edge, and 1 reverse-ambiguous. The search expands if a peak reaches its boundary; accepted points are not filtered by agreement with DIS.

| NCC-to-DIS difference, px | Count | Median | P95 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Mobile matches, measured magnitude > 2 px | 31 | 0.45 | 1.03 |
| Stationary matches, measured magnitude < 0.5 px | 227 | 0.022 | 0.103 |

Four accepted points lie between these classes. These are method differences, not errors against ground truth. Reporting the all-point median would conceal disagreement in the moving part.

A second source-only selection confined to the eyes uses a 15-pixel NCC template instead of the primary 19-pixel template. It accepts **87 of 128** points: 27 ambiguous, 8 reverse-ambiguous, 4 reverse-closure, 1 low-NCC, and 1 low-texture are rejected. Its **53 mobile** matches have median/P95 difference **0.36/2.25 px** against DIS; its **31 stationary** matches have **0.033/0.161 px**. The mobile P99 difference reaches **5.23 px**. These smaller, nearby patches are correlated observations and are not a second independent dataset.

The sensitivity is spatially meaningful. In source-defined central lower-lid bands, DIS–Farneback difference P95 reaches **8.43/8.40 px**, and forward/backward closure reaches **2.26/2.44 px**. Nasal-sclera closure reaches **2.67/4.66 px**. Exact boundary shape, narrow peaks, and disoccluded texture are less secure than the overall direction of expansion. The two dense methods agree on the principal signed lobes but do not justify a precise universal support radius or interpolation formula.

## Sclera, lids, and protected observations

Source-defined local windows were chosen from the visible anatomy before examining match agreement. They are exploratory windows, not semantic masks.

- Upper central lids have upward NCC displacement: **3 accepted points** on the image-left side span **−4.56 to −2.64 px** in dy; **6 points** on the image-right side span **−4.46 to −2.39 px**. This directly contradicts a fixed-lid iris-only warp.
- Lower central lids include downward matches: the left window's **3 points** span **+3.25 to +4.12 px** in dy. The right window's **6 points** span **−1.36 to +4.54 px**, including a conflicting match; that conflict is retained rather than filtered away.
- Temporal sclera has outward horizontal correspondence: **2 left-side points** span **−4.29 to −3.48 px** in dx; **4 right-side points** span **+2.37 to +4.41 px**. Nasal sclera moves in the opposite horizontal direction: **3 left points +2.70 to +5.26 px**, **2 right points −4.20 to −3.20 px**. The support therefore includes more than iris texture.
- Outer-canthus windows have **6 accepted points per eye**, with displacement-magnitude P95 **0.051/0.138 px**. The image-right window is byte-identical; the image-left window has a small changed fringe. This supports quiet outer regions on this pair, not a universal hard endpoint constraint.
- Inner-canthus windows are less protected: **2/3 accepted points**, magnitude P95 **2.89/1.32 px**. Their windows partly intersect the fading nasal support. A strict zero-displacement constraint there would remove observed follow motion.

External texture checks remain quiet: brow/nose/image-left cheek NCC magnitude P95 is **0.060/0.067/0.108 px**, from **59/30/13** points. The image-right cheek has only **2** points, so its apparent stability supplies little independent coverage. Near-zero dense output in weak skin is not treated as proof of exact protection.

## Fact → candidate → exclusions → unknowns

**Fact:** iris-adjacent horizontal components oppose one another, upper/lower components oppose one another, and nearby sclera and lids follow. **Candidate:** a compact local expansion centered near each iris, with separately fitted horizontal/vertical gains and a continuous surrounding falloff. **Excluded as sufficient explanations:** constant eye translation, a rigid motion of the entire eye, and an iris-only binary warp with fixed eyelids.

**Unknown:** the exact center, core shape, support edge, left/right equality, neutral strengths, and treatment of occluded texture. The substantial dense-method sensitivity prevents choosing a unique radial kernel or asserting an exactly circular support. A documented neutral capture, intermediate settings, and additional source-only correspondences at scleral/lid transitions would distinguish a linear core from a narrow boundary emphasis. The [candidate scheme](实现方案.md) keeps these as fitted or unresolved quantities.
