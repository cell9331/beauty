## Chosen mathematical candidate

This implementation uses a separately identified **H2 polynomial candidate**. The earlier general candidate and the locally tested H1 smoothstep family remain hypotheses; neither was recovered as the reference application's mechanism.

For supplied basis columns `R = [axisX axisY]`, local coordinates `v = Rᵀ(p − c)`, radii `rx, ry`, gains `gx, gy` and pair progress `t`:

```text
s       = (v.x/rx)² + (v.y/ry)²
w(s)    = (1 − s)² for s < 1; otherwise 0
D_t(p)  = t R diag(gx, gy) v w(s)
F_t(p)  = p + D_t(p)
```

The compact envelope is continuously differentiable at its boundary. It does not force zero at an eyelid margin or reconstruct unseen material. The specific polynomial is an engineering hypothesis chosen for an inspectable derivative/inverse argument, not a fitted fact or a production default. All portrait-specific values remain in ignored local calibration artifacts.

For `a = max(rx,ry)/min(rx,ry)` and `C = (a + 1/a)/2`, the sufficient global derivative bound is:

```text
||∇D_t|| ≤ t max(|gx|, |gy|) [4C²/(4C − 1)] ||R||² = kappa
```

The implemented certificate bounds the actual supplied basis through the Gram matrix's maximum absolute row sum and rounds arithmetic outward. It does not discard small axis errors accepted by input validation. The support box likewise bounds `R⁻ᵀ diag(rx,ry)` rather than assuming exact orthonormality. Overflow or `kappa >= 1` refuses the affected calibration. For a certified field, global contraction gives a unique inverse; fixed-point evaluation must also meet the explicit residual/iteration policy before any eye is committed.

Each changed destination checks ownership and opacity, then the inverse and **all four** source taps for bounds, ownership and alpha before reading their RGB. One invalid footprint or an unconverged inverse discards the whole affected eye. Exterior pixels remain exact source bytes. No stationary/displaced color blend, donor material or glint restoration is introduced.

The local replay uses an explicit numerical solver budget of 160 iterations and a `1e-6` source-pixel residual. These are replay/inverse settings, separately checked by independent mathematical tests; they are not photographic fit tolerances or recommended production defaults.

## Actual photographic replay and effect verdict

The original `IMG_1939.PNG` → `IMG_1940.PNG` endpoint pair remains on its native aligned grid. The full photograph is 1170 × 1569 pixels; the unchanged analysis crop is 820 × 510. The probe detected one source face once, with the existing production detector. It did not re-detect a deformed result. Stage output cropped back to the analysis grid equals the actual direct Swift output exactly; preview uses that same original-size result before reducing to 329 × 441. Decoded-source parity was checked separately.

The measurement protocol froze source-only texture selection, spatial fit/holdout partition, a template guard band, matching/reverse checks, anatomical reporting groups and known-field controls before the new fits. Of 452 source-selected points (299 fit / 153 holdout), reference NCC accepted 279 (200 / 79) and rejected 173: 116 low-texture, 51 ambiguous, 5 reverse-ambiguous and 1 reverse-closure failures. Rejected and conflicting matches were retained. There are **no accepted iris-interior points** in either partition. Critical held-out lid coverage is still missing: image-left upper/lower lids and image-right lower lid have no accepted held-out matches. The image-right upper lid has only one. Neighboring texture matches are correlated, and a second partition on this already explored portrait does not supply independent new-photo validation.

Mechanical cross-implementation checks were separate: actual Swift forward positions agree with the H2 formula at 452 frozen source positions to a maximum component difference of `1.14e-13 px`; the original OpenCV-based H2 raster and Swift endpoint raster are byte-identical. These checks establish implementation consistency, not reference fidelity.

**Effect verdict: not qualified.** The independent coverage and reference uncertainty do not establish a defensible per-region acceptance budget; the frozen-fit endpoint also remains uncertified and refused under the current sufficient contraction certificate. That refusal does not itself prove actual folding. Known synthetic expansion controls qualify comparator behavior on those controls only. Their residuals and earlier NCC–flow disagreement were not reused as an arbitrary acceptance threshold for this photograph.
