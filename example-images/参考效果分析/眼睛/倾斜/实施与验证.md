### Match the renderer's coordinates before comparing fields

Let `q` use array sample-index coordinates, with the first pixel center at `(0,0)`. The current renderer evaluates its lattice at a half-pixel-shifted geometric coordinate:

```text
B_u(q) = q - d_u(q + (0.5, 0.5))
H(r) = inverse(B_high)(B_low(r))
inverse(H)(q) = inverse(B_low)(B_high(q))
```

`B` maps a destination sample to the immutable source material. `H` therefore moves the same material from the low endpoint to the high endpoint. The raster samples the observed-left source once through `inverse(H)`. It does not subtract endpoint offset arrays at the same index or resample a low-endpoint raster a second time. The two diagnostic carrier-endpoint renders are checked separately against the actual `BeautyStillImageGeometryStage` route.

The test-only solver rejects nonfinite/out-of-domain or clipped coordinates, unsafe declared displacement bounds, failed iteration/residual checks, source-binding mismatch and nonopaque input. It preserves explicit refusal instead of substituting identity. The ordinary-map seam deliberately refuses iris-aperture branches and source clamping; its unsupported cases are not claims about a generalized renderer. A failed raster rolls back the complete research render.

The numerical iteration limit of 256 and residual tolerance of 1e−7 px test comparator convergence. They are not reference-fit tolerances, new production safety caps or appearance acceptance thresholds.

## Retention decision and minimum missing evidence

The observed differences are sufficient to preserve a concrete baseline and focus future investigation. They do not yet justify removing saturation/upper attenuation or replacing the core with newly guessed rotation/support parameters. Retain the existing formula and all failures/limits. G01/G02/G06/G10/G13/G14 are not closed by numerical or public-route tests.

The minimum next evidence package is:

1. Capture a reset, controlled photo with a certified neutral and both tilt endpoints, visible numeric settings and unchanged other controls. Keep the exact source used for source geometry/carrier preparation. This separates observed-endpoint frame assumptions from the actual neutral rendering contract.
2. Preserve source-selected moving core, transition and brow/bridge/cheek protection observations, together with complete matching/reverse/rejection records. Reserve spatially guarded correspondences before fitting; do not reuse already inspected discovery points as a fresh held-out claim.
3. Use known finite-rotation controls and retained small-template sensitivities to freeze full-vector, pair-distance, support/protection and coverage criteria before viewing a replacement. Account for correlation and ambiguous texture; do not turn the current residuals into retrospective pass limits.
4. Replay the unchanged implementation under that frozen contract. Only a distinguishable structured failure can open T3 and identify the smallest component to alter. A candidate must then pass the same comparator and actual raster/protection checks; insufficient evidence keeps the current production behavior.
