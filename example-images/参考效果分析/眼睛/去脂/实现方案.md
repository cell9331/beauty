# Candidate implementation hypothesis

Status: **candidate only**. No SDK code is implemented or validated by this document. This is a geometry hypothesis for the source-relative appearance observed in S0 → `IMG_1938`; the observation does not identify an isolated slider span or certify S0 as neutral.

The separately authorized [2026-10-17 test-only experiment](实施与验证.md) implements only the straight source-frame, constant-normal special case. It does not implement the general curved projection/semantic owner described below or qualify the reference effect. Its explicit local inputs are not production defaults.

The measured upward movement of upper-lid texture motivates a local displacement bump. The UI name 去脂 does not provide evidence for a color, shading, physical volume, or anatomical fat-removal model.

## Required geometry

Use a reliable upper-lid curve `c(s)` in the source image, parameterized by normalized arc length `s`, together with inner/outer canthal references and a superior-oriented unit normal `n(s)`. Choose its sign from image/face orientation, rather than assuming that a fixed screen direction handles every pose. Define a geometric eye scale `L` from the canthal separation. The present analysis does not supply a detector, production landmark indices, or a transferable numerical coefficient.

For a source point `p` in a trusted tubular neighborhood of the upper-lid curve, let `s(p)` be its closest-curve coordinate and `η(p)` its signed distance in the superior normal direction. Reject ambiguous nearest-curve projections or degenerate geometry. Curve orientation, normal variation, and scale must be prepared from the current input, rather than copied from screenshot profile locations.

Iris/sclera, lash detail, eyebrow, lower lid, and neighboring feature regions are useful independent protection observations. Their desired ownership and reliable boundaries must be established separately. The measured field includes surrounding skin and must not be restricted automatically to a binary eyelid mask.

## Minimal forward field

The common coordinate, inverse-mapping, continuity, and one-sample rendering requirements are defined in [the shared implementation conventions](../实现公共约定.md). The item-specific candidate is:

```text
B(t) = (1 − t²)²,  if |t| < 1
       0,          otherwise

b(s) = B((s − s0) / rs)
h(η) = B((η − η0) / rn)
W(p) = b(s(p)) h(η(p)) P(p)

D(p,u) = a(u) W(p) n(s(p))
F_u(p) = p + D(p,u)
```

Here `a(u) ≥ 0` moves texture superiorly, `P(p)` is a continuously varying protection/ownership weight, and `s0`, `η0`, `rs`, and `rn` control the bump center and support. Parameters are source-relative and must be fitted or explicitly chosen as design assumptions. `B` and its first derivative vanish at its support edge, providing a simple continuous connection to stationary skin.

This compact separable bump is a hypothesis, not a recovered closed-source formula. Symmetric normal support is an initial simplification. If independent upper/lower transition correspondences show reproducible asymmetry, replace it with separately fitted smooth transitions and recheck the complete field. Do not add asymmetry merely to chase noisy dense pixels.

The support may extend beyond either canthus. Zero motion at the bump boundary is a candidate design property; **strict fixation at the actual eye corners was not measured**. Set the support from observations and protection requirements rather than forcing a corner to zero because it is an available landmark.

A direction fixed to the local superior axis is also an approximation. If residual dx has a reproducible pattern beyond method differences, test whether normal variation explains it before adding an independent tangential field. The present evidence does not justify an arbitrary horizontal component or a finite rotation model.

## Calibration and unidentified quantities

The sparse moving group supports an upward source-relative magnitude around 1–2 native screenshot pixels in this comparison. This is an observation scale, not an isolated slider amplitude or a default for new images. Dense maxima must not be substituted for a stable calibrated amplitude.

Fit candidate displacement to accepted source-coordinate correspondences, with independent holdout correspondences and region-aware errors. Treat the 31 moving broad-eye correspondences separately from the 52 small-motion correspondences so background zeros cannot dominate the fit. Retain the sparse-versus-dense disagreements and rejection counts.

Normalize an amplitude only after measuring `L` under an explicit definition. The analysis has not established a cross-image `a/L` coefficient. The support, peak position, and transition widths are likewise uncalibrated. Screenshot-specific positions and widths are not production constants.

A future response `a(u) = A r(u)` requires a chosen zero, endpoint mapping, and response function. None is identified by this single observed state. In particular, do not divide the source-relative amplitude by two, impose a symmetric negative control, or label S0 as the zero value. An isolated zero/endpoint sequence is needed before that mapping can be fitted.

## Protection, field safety, and sampling

`P(p)` should resolve effect ownership using reliable local geometry and confidence. It may attenuate motion near independently protected regions. It must not be inferred from the display heat map or used to erase an observed displacement merely because the point falls inside a semantic mask. Inadequate geometry or conflicting protection should fail closed with no effect.

Check the complete spatial derivative, including the bump, protection, coordinate projection, and varying-normal terms:

```text
∇D = a [ n ⊗ ∇W + W ∇n ]
J_F = I + ∇D
```

The terms hidden inside `∇W` include derivatives through both `s(p)` and `η(p)`, and through `P(p)`. A safe center amplitude does not establish a safe transition. Bound or verify the full field on its support, retain margin from singularity, and check global coverage/injectivity as required by the shared conventions. A positive sampled determinant alone does not prove a globally one-to-one map.

Rendering must invert `F_u`; a single evaluation of `q − D(q,u)` is not generally the exact inverse. Use a verified inverse solver or field representation, test its residual, and sample the original image once. Do not repeatedly deform the previous output or blend two misregistered outputs to hide seams. Handle projection, inverse-solve, coverage, and confidence failures with an unchanged result in the affected operation.

## Falsifiable evaluation

1. Obtain a controlled zero and endpoint capture on the same photograph. If their displacement differs materially from S0 → `IMG_1938`, relabel and refit the source-relative candidate before claiming a control mapping.
2. Fit using only a designated subset of reliable moving correspondences. Test the held-out moving points and independent small-motion points separately, including signed dx and dy. A visually plausible heat map is insufficient.
3. Check along-lid and cross-lid profiles with reliable texture points. A measured flat core would favor a translated plateau; a localized peak favors a bump. A stable two-sided opposite-sign pattern would contradict the current one-direction model.
4. Add independent iris, lower-lid, corner, eyebrow, and surrounding-skin windows. Detectable protected-region movement or abrupt texture seams would reject the chosen support/protection design. The current forehead/nose controls certify only those sampled windows and their observed residuals.
5. Compare the candidate's source-point forward vectors with the measured source-relative vectors. Do not subtract fields on unrelated pixel grids. Keep field-fit residual, method difference, inverse residual, and visual appearance qualification distinct.
6. Run synthetic zero, superior-translation, and compact-field checks appropriate to the proposed geometry and scale. Verify signs, full derivatives, inverse consistency, and texture continuity. Such checks validate the candidate mathematics, not reproduction of the reference product.

These are acceptance criteria for a future implementation hypothesis. This analysis does not create an SDK execution plan, download a model, or imply that the appearance has already been reproduced.
