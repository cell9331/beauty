## Objective and current boundary

Investigate whether an independently owned, source-anchored superior displacement of upper-lid texture can reproduce a **controlled** reference adjustment. This is a new geometric hypothesis derived from the current measurements. It is not a revival of the historical tone/frequency or interior-warp experiments, and it is not a promise of anatomical fat removal or a natural-appearance improvement.

The present S0 → `IMG_1938` comparison resolves localized superior texture movement, with accepted moving correspondences around 1–2 native screenshot pixels. S0 is not a certified neutral state. That observation cannot assign the change exclusively to 去脂, define a slider response, or supply a production `a/L` coefficient. The candidate may be rejected by a controlled pair before any product route is changed.

The current SDK scalar `upperEyelidFullnessReduction` still selects bounded brightness-relief processing through [BeautyEngine](../../../../BeautySDK/Sources/BeautySDK/BeautyEngine.swift#L439), [the relief editor](../../../../BeautySDK/Sources/BeautyEffects/LocalRetouch/BeautyUpperEyelidFullnessEditor.swift#L161) and [its luminance model](../../../../BeautySDK/Sources/BeautyEffects/LocalRetouch/BeautyUpperEyelidReliefModel.swift#L18). Those operations preserve source coordinates. They do not implement this geometric hypothesis. Keep their explicit-call compatibility and existing failure behavior; their mechanical tests do not establish the new effect.

Keep `eye.fatRemoval` unbound (`field: nil`) in [ToolCatalog](../../../../BeautyEditorApp/Sources/ToolCatalog.swift#L33), retain the suspended status, and keep the retained scalar out of default renderer batches. Do not bind this tool to `eyeHeight`, `upperEyelidLift`, brow movement, global smoothing, lower-lid lowering or another existing control.

The current [repository entry](../../../../README.md) and [host validation record](../../../../BeautyEditorApp/VALIDATION.md) govern the repository boundary. Historical acceptance, plans and research links mentioned by the local spike skill are unavailable in this checkout; they are not current gates and must not be restored to make this plan executable. Authorized generated portraits and authorized photographs are eligible for the new protocol. Input provenance alone does not qualify an effect.

### Capture and independent evaluation protocol

1. Preserve the current source-relative result as a separate observation. Capture explicit zero/off, endpoint and repeated zero/off for one controlled input, recording crop, scale, selected control, visible settings, side scope and any background-protection state. Reset other adjustments explicitly. A supplied original alone is not the zero capture.
2. Before matching targets, select stable registration texture outside the eye/brow neighborhood, independent registration holdouts, and source-texture moving/protection windows. Fit only the global display transform. Expand searches that reach their boundary; retain rejection counts and weak-texture uncertainty. Keep moving and stationary errors separate.
3. Freeze at least one fitting positive and **two independent held-out positives**, plus at least **two appearance negatives** with resolved upper-lid texture but no independently defined target adjustment. These counts are proposed protocol minima, not measured adequacy or a population claim. Split by source image, not by patches from the same source. 4. Add invalid-support controls separately: missing/closed/blinking/occluded eyes, ambiguous lid projection, malformed contours, low-confidence ownership and one valid eye with one invalid peer. These are admission negatives, not substitutes for appearance negatives. Generated inputs may exercise these cases when independently reviewable and authorized.
5. ## New field and a test that distinguishes it from aliases

Use the [item candidate](实现方案.md) and [shared coordinate/inverse conventions](../实现公共约定.md). Prepare a source upper-lid curve `c(s)`, normalized arc length `s`, a superior unit normal `n(s)`, signed normal distance `η`, and a positive source canthal scale `L`. Choose the normal from upright source/face orientation; negative screen-y is only the current photograph's superior direction. Reject self-overlap or ambiguous nearest-curve projections rather than selecting an arbitrary branch.

The initial low-parameter hypothesis is:

```text
B(t) = (1 - t²)² for |t| < 1, otherwise 0
W(p) = B((s(p)-s0)/rs) B((η(p)-η0)/rn) P(p)
D(p,u) = a(u) W(p) n(s(p))
F_u(p) = p + D(p,u)
∇D = a(u) [ n ⊗ ∇W + W ∇n ]
```

`a(u)` has source-pixel units and is nonnegative for the initial superior hypothesis. Its endpoint, response and normalized `a/L` remain to be fitted after the evidence gate. Centers, widths and `P` are source-derived or explicitly provisional choices. No screenshot profile location, fixed radius, 1–2 px amplitude or historical experimental coefficient becomes a new-input constant.

Start with this bump and compare a translated plateau only if independent profiles discriminate them. Do not add free tangential components, asymmetric supports or extra lobes merely to reduce dense-flow noise. Full derivatives include curve projection, protection, normal variation and all transitions. A zero at the chosen support boundary does not establish zero at a real eye corner.

The independent discriminator must compare the same held-out source points against three separately rendered candidates: this new field, current `upperEyelidLift`, and current `eyeHeight`, each under a declared matching objective and admissible budget. Report both components, along-/cross-lid profiles, visible upper-edge movement, lower-lid response, iris/lash/brow following and surrounding-skin decay. The proposed field must earn its separate identity through those observations; a different class name or a nonzero pixel count is insufficient. If the evidence cannot distinguish it from an existing control, do not expose a separate accepted 去脂 effect or silently choose an alias.

## Support ownership, rendering and local failure

Create an independent geometry-support owner. It may reuse current observed eye/face coordinates and validation primitives, but must not reuse brightness convexity as proof of geometric tissue ownership. Treat the existing relief `SemanticAnalyzer` and its approvals as owned by the compatibility route. A new owner prepares a trusted source curve/tube, confidence, continuous protection, source coverage and per-eye admission once per request. If current landmarks cannot establish the required region, abstain; do not infer a semantic boundary from the heat map or a binary eye outline.

Reuse the immutable [canonical still-image carrier](../../../../BeautySDK/Sources/BeautyCore/Models/BeautyCanonicalStillImage.swift) and current orientation/color handling through [the canonicalizer](../../../../BeautySDK/Sources/BeautySDK/BeautyStillImageCanonicalizer.swift). The current direct local route requires exact-opaque bounded RGB. Preserve that refusal for this first geometric route: do not flatten transparent input or turn its rejection into a successful no-op. Preserve output alpha bytes on admitted inputs. Broader alpha support would need its own source-coverage/sampling design and tests; it is not implied here.

Reuse the request-local ownership and immutable-source principles of [local composition](../../../../BeautySDK/Sources/BeautyEffects/Render/BeautyLocalRetouchComposition.swift), not its RGB-proposal representation as a fake geometry map. The new coordinate owner must explicitly resolve overlaps, sibling protection and source domains. Initially admit one geometric effect in isolation; reject unqualified combinations with an explicit route result. Then compose inverse **coordinates** with directly affected eye/face stages before original-source sampling. Appending another already-resampled raster to the current geometry-stage order does not meet this plan.

Certify the complete source field and combined transitions. Use a verified global derivative bound sufficient for injectivity/contraction, or a separately justified representation with coverage and non-overlap checks. Include every term of `∇W` and `∇n`; sampled determinant positivity alone is insufficient. Solve the inverse with bounded iterations and verify the real source-pixel residual, then sample immutable source RGB once. Test outside-support exact identity and interpolation-footprint containment. Do not blend two misregistered renderings to hide a seam.

Missing/malformed/uncertain ownership, unsafe field, failed inverse, unavailable source coverage or conflicting overlap returns identity for that eye operation. Preserve a valid peer when ownership is independent; if a coupled composition cannot be certified, refuse that composition rather than dropping a few pixels into a discontinuous mixed field. Input format/resource errors retain their explicit error category. Diagnostics expose only allowlisted aggregate admission/refusal and cost data, with no raw pixels, masks, curves, correspondences or private paths.

## Gates and stop conditions

1. **Evidence gate:** G01/G04/G05/G14 close sufficiently to specify a falsifiable control target and independent validation set. Without this, only foundational synthetic/compatibility work can finish.
2. **Mechanism gate:** the compact superior field explains held-out signed correspondences and support better than the declared alias baselines under frozen criteria. Reject it if the controlled pair lacks the predicted motion, needs unsupported mask ownership, or cannot be distinguished as a separate effect.
3. **Safety/source gate:** full-field certificate, inverse residual, source coverage, protection, request isolation, unchanged invalid peer and current input contract pass. Do not increase gain or relax protection to hide a failed effect.
4. **Integration gate:** explicit route separation, old-call compatibility and G13 coordinate composition/cost pass. Do not enable a sequential-raster fallback merely because isolated rendering passed.
5. **Effect gate:** independent held-out positives and appearance negatives pass their frozen vector, visual and protection criteria at the declared scales. More unit tests, accepted support, darker pixels or safe output cannot replace this gate.
6. **Product gate:** only a separately authorized implementation/integration task may expose the qualified route. Until then the feature remains suspended and the host field remains nil.

Stop and retain the failed verdict when the new evidence contradicts the hypothesis, the fixed holdouts fail, the dedicated behavior collapses to an alias, or the useful motion requires unsafe ownership/gradient/cost. Do not restart the historical relief/interior-warp routes, broaden inputs, alter frozen criteria or reopen model research to manufacture a passing result. Record aggregate findings and the smallest new discriminating evidence needed for any subsequent decision.
