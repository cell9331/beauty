Baseline adapters, candidate mathematics, synthetic counterexamples, compatibility checks and document work can proceed where listed. A prototype passing mechanical checks does not close a reference-effect gap. None of the proposed captures, tests, thresholds or implementation work has been completed by writing this register.

## G01 — Neutral, endpoint identity and response

**Known:** eleven screenshot pairs show observed left/right endpoints without numbers. No common application neutral or complete reset state is certified. 去脂 has one selected state only.

**Missing / next evidence:** same-source captures with recorded active control, all other states, an explicit zero/reset, both available endpoints and selected intermediate positions. Establish display alignment independently. Repeat a zero after the sequence to detect accumulation or state drift. For one-sided controls, record the real off/zero and enabled range rather than inventing a negative endpoint.

**Blocks:** measured neutral-relative coefficients, symmetric half-amplitudes, a reference-certified slider response and claims of full UI-range fidelity. **Can proceed:** direct pair-relative comparison, source-relative discovery for 去脂, and preservation/testing of the existing centered SDK slider as a product contract. An explicitly chosen product mapping must remain labelled a design choice.

<a id="g02"></a>
## G02 — Core shape, support and protected transitions

**Known:** signed component patterns distinguish several broad mechanisms; source-selected stationary windows constrain selected remote regions. Dense fields disagree most at narrow transitions and weak/occluded texture.

**Missing / next evidence:** source-selected moving, transition and stationary texture covering both axes and all relevant sides; repeat at a second template scale/method; inspect native texture/contours; retain disagreement and rejected counts. Fit a small internal model before fitting its independent outer envelope. A reliable flat segment versus a local peak must decide plateau versus bump.

**Blocks:** exact support radii, hard anatomical zeros, unique kernels and universal asymmetric widths. **Can proceed:** low-parameter candidates and current-baseline comparisons. Fixed support constants may be provisional engineering choices; they cannot be called recovered reference parameters.

<a id="g03"></a>
## G03 — Iris geometry, lid following and occlusion

**Known:** the iris-size pair has independent evidence of central upper/lower lid and adjacent scleral motion. Current fixed-aperture behavior conflicts with that observation. Outer canthal texture is quieter; the nasal transition is not uniformly fixed.

**Missing / next evidence:** reliable source iris center/scale uncertainty, following-lid and scleral transition coverage, and qualified positive/negative examples for partial iris occlusion, lashes, narrow/closed eyes and source-coverage loss. Establish what the geometric field can render without inventing disoccluded material.

**Blocks:** a production support mask, unique circular/elliptical core, unseen-material reconstruction and generalization beyond the measured pair. **Can proceed:** a geometry-only candidate with observed lid/sclera following, controlled shape/occlusion cases and fail-closed ownership tests. Do not preserve the old fixed-lid target merely to keep its tests green, or delete independent safety assertions to allow arbitrary leakage.

## G04 — Isolated 去脂 displacement

**Known:** aligned S0 → IMG_1938 supports roughly 1–2 screenshot pixels of upper-lid upward texture motion above the stable registration residual. S0 is same-source content, not a certified application zero.

**Missing / next evidence:** a documented same-source off/zero → selected endpoint sequence with other effects reset, a repeated zero, and sufficient independent upper-lid/adjacent texture correspondences. Compare the isolated pair with the existing source-relative map and record any disagreement.

**Blocks:** assigning that amplitude exclusively to 去脂, converting it to a production coefficient and claiming isolated-control reproduction. **Can proceed:** define the new geometric hypothesis, compare the current brightness route, build a separate non-shipping geometry prototype and synthetic sign/inverse tests. If a qualified isolated pair does not support the proposed upward local field, reject or narrow that hypothesis; do not force a 1–2 px target.

## G05 — 去脂 applicability, ownership and a distinct useful effect

**Known:** current brightness relief is suspended in the host; a safer luminance proxy is not evidence of geometric fullness reduction. A new geometric candidate must not simply alias eye height, generic upper-lid lift, smoothing or under-eye treatment.

**Missing / next evidence:** frozen authorized positive and negative examples, reliable upper-lid/crease/skin support and protected anatomy, examples that distinguish the intended localized effect from generic aperture opening, original-detail appearance review, and closed/blinking/occluded/uncertain-support counterexamples. Authorized generated or genuine images can be used; generated fixtures prove only the independently checked criteria they actually pass.

**Blocks:** useful-effect qualification, default discovery/host re-enablement and semantic replacement of the retained public parameter. **Can proceed:** a separate still-only experimental route, private request-local ownership, compatibility tests and mechanical rejection checks. Failed suitable-source tests remain failures. No model or dataset download, historical route revival or public API change follows from this planning request.

## G06 — Tilt core rigidity, angle and pivot

**Known:** small-template correspondences favor approximately opposite 8–9° pairwise rotations over vertical-only shear on source-defined cores; template bias remains. The current two-component inverse-offset field is not a pure vertical shear.

**Missing / next evidence:** same-source endpoint maps for the current implementation, rigid/affine/shear residuals and pairwise-distance changes on the same independently selected core points; a separately observed decay band; sensitivity to template size and core selection. Fix a source reference center and fit translation, or test a constrained pivot, without claiming both are uniquely measured.

**Blocks:** a uniquely identified pivot, precision angle, forced zero translation and a claim that every current linear rotational term must be rewritten. **Can proceed:** the baseline comparator, finite-rotation candidate and full-vector synthetic oracles. If current pairwise behavior already meets the frozen core criterion, retain it rather than rewriting only because its formula lacks sin/cos. Do not halve the observed angle span into measured one-sided rotations.

<a id="g07"></a>
## G07 — Lower-lid peak and weak moving coverage

**Known:** the outer lower-lid region moves inferiorly. The primary mobile NCC check has four points and P95 disagreement of 12.33 px; denser/multiple-template checks remain sensitive.

**Missing / next evidence:** a better-supported same-source effect pair and source-only moving texture along the lower lid, transition and protected iris/upper lid. Determine whether mismatches reflect repeated texture, occlusion, deformation or inadequate patch support before trusting a peak.

**Blocks:** amplitude retuning to dense maxima, precise bump width and exact reference dx. **Can proceed:** preserve current safe localized behavior, instrument a private comparator, test direction/ownership and prepare a curved-normal alternative. More stationary points do not close the missing mobile evidence.

<a id="g08"></a>
## G08 — Outer-eye lift: bump versus pivoted rotation

**Known:** temporal texture moves up while much of the inner region is quiet. Quiet inner texture does not exclude a rigid rotation around a nearby nasal pivot.

**Missing / next evidence:** full dx/dy and same-core rigid/pairwise fits over distributed source points, plus surrounding decay. Compare current vertical lift, compact local bump and pivoted rotation on identical accepted observations.

**Blocks:** a uniquely selected internal model and evidence-based deletion/addition of a tangential component. **Can proceed:** candidate comparisons and preservation of existing protection. A small dx near correspondence uncertainty does not automatically justify a new free parameter.

<a id="g09"></a>
## G09 — Canthal core shape and orthogonal motion

**Known:** inner canthi move nasally, outer canthi temporally. Independent mobile-corner coverage is sparse, with small dy and method-sensitive transitions.

**Missing / next evidence:** independent corner and adjacent-sclera texture across the active core and fade, multiple template scales, source eye-axis orientation and repeated observations. Separate motion explained by the local tangent from a residual normal component.

**Blocks:** exact plateau versus bump, hard iris-fringe fixation and a calibrated independent vertical gain. **Can proceed:** current-baseline comparison, local-axis candidates and source-defined protection tests. Neither adding a vertical term nor preserving exact image-y zero is a demonstrated reference solution yet.

<a id="g10"></a>
## G10 — Pose and direction semantics

**Known:** current vertical-position/spacing and several localized terms use screen axes; document candidates often use source eye/face axes. The supplied near-frontal batch does not identify the intended pose behavior.

**Missing / next evidence:** qualified same-control references with head roll or oblique eye orientation; distinguish face-level horizontal/inferior axes from each eye's canthal tangent. Synthetic rotations can test a chosen covariance contract, but do not establish that the reference product uses it.

**Blocks:** claiming a local-axis replacement is required by the reference and general pose fidelity. **Can proceed:** explicit axis contracts, frozen current-behavior tests, candidate rotated-input tests and frontal calibration. Change the production axis only when the target decision is justified; do not silently redefine the existing slider.

## G11 — Four implemented menu-only targets

**Known:** 提肌、眼神矫正、对称、祛红血丝 have SDK implementations and unavailable host controls. No selected reference effect pair was supplied for them.

**Missing / next evidence:** independently qualified state pairs and target-specific criteria: lid motion for 提肌; intended gaze direction/target for 眼神矫正; reference side/averaging/spacing behavior for 对称; managed-color red-material and protected-tissue evidence for 祛红血丝. A generic displacement oracle cannot qualify redness.

**Blocks:** reference-fidelity claims, semantic rewrites from their names and host enablement justified as reference completion. **Can proceed:** retain compatibility/safety, document current behavior and prepare feature-specific capture/analysis protocols. Existing implementation is not evidence of the reference mechanism.

## G13 — Composition, backend behavior and cost

**Known:** ordinary continuous eye terms share one eye pass, while dense iris and other geometry stages can resample sequentially. The document proposes source-coordinate composition and one final source sample. Actual quality/cost of a replacement has not been measured.

**Missing / next evidence:** bounded eye-only composition tests, coordinate/ownership compatibility, inverse closure, actual sampled texture, retained CPU/Metal behavior and incremental runtime/memory measurements on affected paths. Add other-stage combinations only when they are affected dependencies. Still-image results do not qualify realtime or target-device performance.

**Blocks:** combined-route rollout, unqualified speed/memory claims and replacing the entire SDK geometry architecture under this plan. **Can proceed:** an eye-scoped coordinate adapter/compositor and targeted single-control comparisons. Preserve current unrelated stages and explicitly restrict any new route to combinations it has qualified.

<a id="g14"></a>
## G14 — Frozen effect-fit and protection acceptance

**Known:** NCC, DIS and Farneback have measurable disagreement; current tests cover substantial synthetic response and safety behavior, not this batch's independent endpoint-vector residuals.

**Missing / next evidence:** a versioned local protocol frozen before fitting: source-state identity, scale, moving/transition/protection selection, acceptance/rejection rules, fit/holdout partition, per-region dx/dy and contour metrics, minimum reliable coverage, target uncertainty, candidate acceptance bounds and stop conditions. Include suitable independent positives and negatives, not only texture already used to choose the candidate.

**Blocks:** declaring reference reproduction or using repeated tuning as acceptance. **Can proceed:** a comparator and known-field controls. Method-difference P95 is not ground-truth error and must not automatically become an allowed reproduction residual. Engineering inverse/derivative budgets must be identified separately from photographic fit bounds. Freeze remaining bounds before parameter optimization and preserve failed outcomes rather than changing the oracle afterward.

**Iris execution result:** source-only guarded selection, fit/holdout partition, matching checks and controls were frozen and replayed. Accepted held-out counts remain zero for image-left upper/lower lids and image-right lower lid, and one for image-right upper lid. This does not support broad lid-follow acceptance or a photographic tolerance. The comparator and actual outputs are retained; G14 remains open rather than weakening partition/coverage rules after seeing the fit.
