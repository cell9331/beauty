# Inner-canthus analysis chain

## Question and qualified comparison

The question is whether the control moves the whole eye, stretches the entire eye opening, or changes only its nasal end. The measured comparison is `IMG_1947.PNG` → `IMG_1948.PNG`, observed left → right slider endpoint. The photo grid is unchanged; original crop bytes are preserved, without resampling or a deformable registration. Other controls' reset state and a common neutral are not certified.

The source-to-target field is `D(p) = p_target − p_source`, with positive x right and positive y down, in screenshot pixels. External brow/nose/cheek texture is reserved for stability checks. Iris interiors, both canthi, and the sclera on each side are inspected independently. UI and photo overlays are excluded. Native eye crops were reviewed in addition to the evidence overview.

## Facts from the signed fields

The image-left nasal corner moves right and the image-right nasal corner moves left. The principal components therefore point toward the nasal bridge. The dominant response is horizontal, with a much smaller and less stable vertical component. The largest estimated movement lies around the nasal corner, rather than at the iris center. Adjacent nasal sclera and a fringe of the lower lid follow; the temporal side remains quiet.

## Independent checks and rejection accounting

Primary source-only NCC accepts **272 of 383** candidates. Rejections are **74 low-texture, 19 ambiguous, 17 edge, and 1 reverse-ambiguous**. Search-boundary peaks are rechecked with a larger search, and accepted matches are not filtered by DIS agreement.

| NCC-to-DIS difference, px | Count | Median | P95 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Mobile, measured magnitude > 2 px | 8 | 0.85 | 2.28 |
| Stationary, measured magnitude < 0.5 px | 263 | 0.022 | 0.097 |

One accepted point lies between these classes. A small all-point median would be dominated by stationary background and is not evidence for subpixel accuracy at the corner.

A supplementary source-only eye selection, using a 15-pixel template rather than 19 pixels, accepts **109 of 130** candidates. It rejects **17 ambiguous, 3 reverse-ambiguous, and 1 low-texture**. Its **9 mobile** points have median/P95 difference **0.76/2.63 px** from DIS; **98 stationary** points have **0.028/0.130 px**. Two lie between classes. Nearby patches are correlated, and this supplement is a sensitivity check rather than an independent dataset.

The source-defined active-canthus windows each contain only **one accepted supplementary match**: image-left **dx +14.80, dy +0.26 px**; image-right **dx −15.90, dy +0.69 px**. These support the direction and motion scale at those textures. They do not prove that the full canthus translates rigidly, that its core is a plateau, or that vertical displacement is exactly zero. An additional nasal-sclera match has a larger orthogonal component, so the vertical field should not be fitted to only the two favorable corner points.

The two dense methods agree that the nasal side is active but differ strongly in amplitude and width. Weak skin, local stretching, and finite template support can influence the inferred boundary. There is not enough evidence to attach a precise support radius or transition width to this control.

## Iris and opposite-corner protection

Source-defined iris-interior windows have **5/3 supplementary accepted points** and displacement-magnitude P95 **0.132/0.041 px**. The image-right window is byte-identical between endpoints; the image-left window has a changed fringe covering **6.1%** of its pixels. This supports quiet iris interiors and warns against declaring the complete iris region mathematically fixed.

Temporal-canthus windows are byte-identical and have **6/5 accepted points**, with displacement-magnitude P95 **0.056/0.064 px**. Temporal-sclera windows are also byte-identical. Central upper-lid windows are byte-identical, while lower-lid windows have changed fringes. The field's support thus involves the nasal neighborhood, rather than a binary canthal point or the full eye.

External brow/nose/image-left cheek NCC magnitude P95 is **0.057/0.066/0.108 px**, from **58/30/13** accepted points. Image-right cheek coverage is only **2 points**, and does not establish broad protection on that side. Dense zeros in weak texture are not promoted to independent observations.

## Fact → candidate → exclusions → unknowns

**Fact:** opposite nasal horizontal motions are concentrated at the inner ends, while iris interiors and outer corners are quiet. **Candidate:** a compact local corner displacement with continuous falloff across nasal sclera and nearby lid/skin. **Excluded as sufficient explanations:** whole-eye translation, uniform scaling of the full eye, and a rigid rotation of the whole eye. Each would move the independently quiet regions.

**Unknown:** exact core plateau versus bump, support anisotropy, small vertical behavior, complete iris-boundary protection, neutral decomposition, and intermediate response. The [outer-canthus pair](../外眼角/分析链路.md) activates the opposite end, distinguishing this control from a general eye-length control. More source-only corner/transition correspondences and a documented neutral/intermediate capture would constrain the remaining shape. The [candidate model](实现方案.md) must retain these limitations.
