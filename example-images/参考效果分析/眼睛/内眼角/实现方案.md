# Inner-canthus candidate field

[Measured evidence](分析链路.md) · [Shared mathematics and rendering](../实现公共约定.md)

Status: **candidate only; no SDK implementation or reference validation**. The forward field is proposed to model the observed endpoint-span direction and locality. It does not identify the closed-source algorithm.

## Local geometry and model

For each eye, obtain a source nasal-canthus anchor `c`, an eye-aligned horizontal direction `e_h`, its downward normal `e_v`, and eye-derived length scales. Let `e_n` be `e_h` or `−e_h`, chosen to point toward the nasal bridge. The local shape and its orientation must follow the input geometry rather than fixed screen coordinates.

Define `r = p − c`, `s = r·e_n`, `t = r·e_v`, and a compact source-space support coordinate:

```text
ρ²        = (s/R_s)² + (t/R_t)²
W(p)      = (max(0, 1 − ρ²))²
D_pair(p) = W(p) [A e_n + B e_v]
F_τ(p)    = p + τ D_pair(p),   0 ≤ τ ≤ 1
```

The squared compact bump is a simple C1 **design candidate**, not a measured kernel. Its core could be replaced by a short plateau only if additional independent correspondences support one. `A > 0` is chosen to model inward nasal-corner movement in the observed left-to-right span; `B` is reserved for independently fitting a small orthogonal response and must not be inferred by rotating a nominally horizontal vector along a steep local canthal slope.

`τ` interpolates between the observed source and target in this model. It is neither the application's measured slider mapping nor a neutral-centered strength. The source/target pair does not justify halving `A` to assign two neutral endpoint amplitudes.

## Fit the active corner and its fade

Use the measured corner movement scale, approximately **15–16 px at the two supplementary matched corner textures**, as an initial constraint for this screenshot pair. Dense inner-canthus-window P95 magnitudes of **17.46/16.34 px** provide a different, method-sensitive summary; neither is a universal amplitude or exact global maximum.

Fit `A`, `B`, `R_s`, and `R_t` to mobile correspondences and the nasal transition. Keep both eyes separately fitted until equal strength is supported. The support must include adjacent nasal sclera and the observed lower-lid fringe. It should fade before the quiet iris interior and temporal corner. A support parameter may be asymmetric toward skin and toward iris only if its benefit is supported by transition correspondences, rather than by dense-flow noise.

If a smooth protection weight is required around the iris or opposite corner, calibrate it against the measured quiet windows. The image-left iris fringe has a small change, so protecting the entire iris boundary as exactly zero is an unverified design restriction. The active patch must not accidentally move the nasal bridge or the opposite eye.

## Full-field safety and rendering

For a constant local displacement vector `v = A e_n + B e_v`, the unprotected derivative is `∇D = v ∇Wᵀ`. Full-field safety therefore depends on the falloff gradient, not merely on the translation amplitude. If a protection factor is added, its derivative must be included through the product rule. The displacement decays to zero with zero first derivative at the compact boundary, but this alone does not prevent folding inside the fade.

Follow the shared full Jacobian, injectivity, inverse residual, and single-sample requirements. Reject or reduce the candidate if geometry is uncertain, the support cannot connect to protected regions without excessive compression, or the inverse cannot be resolved. Do not assume that one subtraction of the forward field gives the exact inverse.

## What would validate or falsify it

Check the two nasal directions, corner motion scale, transition through nasal sclera, quiet iris interior, quiet temporal canthus, and external texture independently. A broad translational residual at the iris would reject the chosen support. A flat measured core would favor a platform over the current bump. Consistent vertical residuals would require revising `B` or the local basis, rather than silently filtering those points.

The current active correspondence count is small and dense-method disagreement reaches several pixels at the corner. A future reproduction claim needs better target qualification and actual rendered source-to-target verification; mathematical continuity alone is insufficient.
