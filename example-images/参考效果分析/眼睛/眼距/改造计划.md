## Current behavior and change decision

Each core translates outward on the image-horizontal axis, with a shared normalized coefficient, a larger support and explicit bridge/peer protection.

Preserve bilateral core translation. Qualify the direction contract, normalization and bridge-side composition separately; a left/right pixel difference can arise from different source spans.

Current change location: [BeautyContinuousEyeMap.swift](../../../../BeautySDK/Sources/BeautyEffects/Render/BeautyContinuousEyeMap.swift#L594); inspect only the term and shared helpers actually affected.

## Ordered tasks

1. Use C0 to measure each core vector, intra-eye distance preservation and relative separation increase. Inspect bridge, peer, brow and nasal transitions independently.
2. Compare image-horizontal, face-horizontal and each-eye tangent candidates under G10. Fit eyes independently before testing a shared normalized response; keep response assumptions separate from G01 evidence.
3. If a change is supported, isolate the axis/gain/support term. Audit the sum or declared composition where supports meet the bridge; do not silently renormalize overlap weights or stretch an eye to increase spacing.

Use the shared C0 comparator before fitting; C1 field/inverse rules and C2 ownership/composition apply only to changes actually selected. Do not silently change centered slider, serialized scalar or unavailable-host semantics. A field-coefficient change must be evaluated through the actual admitted forward/relative map, including pose and safety scaling.
