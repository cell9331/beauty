# Local Fixture Authorization Boundary

Current superseding policy: [owner-authorized generated portrait-like images
may supply full positive/negative SDK effect evidence](../docs/IMAGE_EFFECT_ACCEPTANCE.md).
Genuine human portraits are optional. The historical feature-specific review
core and fixture decisions below retain their original meaning; their old
`mechanics_only` or AI-generated labels do not impose a new real-photo gate.

This document records the repository policy for local portrait fixtures. It
does not record subject identity, source metadata, file locations, image bytes,
authorization identifiers, or detailed rights terms.

## Policy

- Local fixtures may be used only for the explicitly authorized internal
  evaluation scope and must remain Git-ignored, owner-controlled, and
  non-distributed.
- The SDK is owner-only. No fixture, derivative, target, output, checkpoint, or
  compiled model may be published, sold, delivered to a customer, or transferred
  outside the owner-controlled environment. Any future scope change requires a
  fresh authorization and license audit; current permission does not carry over.
- Authorization permits use but does not assign feature polarity or product
  weight. A feature requires its own opaque positive/negative manifest rows,
  complete original/mask/after assets, predeclared target policy, and the
  structured review required by the owning evidence core.
- Durable evidence may contain only the owning serializer's positive-allowlist
  projection: opaque IDs, feature/polarity, fixed judgments and reasons, and
  aggregate decisions. Paths, media, hashes, rights carriers, reviewer
  identity, timestamps, raw support, geometry, pixels, and freeform text must
  remain local or ephemeral.
- Revoked or unclassified material fails closed and contributes no admission
  weight. An image, generated or genuine, cannot satisfy another feature's
  gate without that feature's own predeclared positive/negative and output
  oracle; a historical mechanics-only decision is not retroactively promoted.

## Retention and Change Control

Raw and generated local files are disposable and must be removed when
permission is revoked. Any change to this boundary must be reflected in the
owning phase contract and verified with the private resolver, serializer,
tracked/staged privacy scan, and the relevant fail-closed tests.

## Disabled Fixtures

Parked or historical fixtures are not authorized as current inputs and must
remain outside active discovery. Historical planning references do not
reactivate them.
